Member of the [[Seekers]].



Nog a Half-Orc Barbarian. He is 2,10m tall and weighs around 105kg in the morning and about 115kg after some chicken wings or five pints of ale. His skin is as dark as his eyes and his hair even darker. The last time he's taken a bath he was still inside his mother's womb, which adds up to a strong smell of rottenness and virility. I wouldn't cross his path, however it may be; not touch his friends, nor foes, for that matter; and specially, whatever happens, I wouldn't dare messing with any one of the two humps of his camel. That being said, Nog is also an avid gambler and a Grand Master in the game of Kings, Queens & Rooks.
