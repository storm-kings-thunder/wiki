![[Silvanus.png|140x197]]
The God of Nature and one of the oldest deities in the world. Many druids pay him homage, but true clerics of his faith are few. This is not due to any weakness in the faith; rather, Silvanus carefully chooses individuals who embody the values upheld by each of his four aspects: the wisdom of the Eagle, the perseverance of the Bull, the ferocity of the Lion, and the ingenuity of Humankind.

Known followers of Silvanus include [[Archdruid Oloamin]] and [[Ellora Oak|Ellora]] of the [[Seekers]].

In 1486 DR, he granted [[Ellora Oak|Ellora]] the Woodland’s Heart, a powerful staff that enhances her abilities, as a reward for cleansing the [[Neverwinter Woods]] of a terrible corruption.
