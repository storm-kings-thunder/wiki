Known as Razi Martison in his human disguise, Trianzim is a powerful General from the Blood War who defected to escape the never-ending battle and bloodshed. For a few decades, he has been living disguised as a shady merchant, constantly attempting to stay discreet until his former allies stop hunting him.

He appeared disguised in the Gold Canary when the [[Seekers]] were there.

In 1486 DR, he made a blood pact with the Seeker Zenvalros. By giving him a small sample of his own blood (an ingredient needed to activate a planar portal), Zenvalros agreed to do an unspecified favor in the near future.

Recently, Trianzim summoned Zenvalros to cash in on his favor. The favor is to guard the mysterious contents of a small container with his life, and to prevent anybody else from opening or stealing it. Whatever the contents are, Trianzim seems to think it is essential to stop being hunted for desertion.

Fiend male. Birthplace: The Nine Hells. Alignment: Lawful Evil.
