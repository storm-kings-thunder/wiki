Laeral Silverhand is one of the Seven Sisters — the seven daughters of [[Mystra, God of Magic|Mystra]] — and one of her most powerful Chosen. Born in 765 DR, she is a legendary archmage who wields silver fire, a destructive and restorative manifestation of raw magical power.

In the late 15th century DR, Laeral serves as the Open Lord of [[Waterdeep]], the public face of the city's ruling council. She previously worked alongside her partner Khelben "Blackstaff" Arunsun to lead the Moonstars, a dissident faction of the [[Harpers]].

Her sister Alustriel Silverhand rules [[Silverymoon]].
