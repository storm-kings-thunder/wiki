Alkai Tor is a small village built around a sacred granite tor on the shores of [[Lake Alkai]]. Founded by wood elves, it later became home to humans, half-elves, and a single dwarf. Its population fell from around fifty to fewer than twenty residents in the three months following the events of 1486 DR.

The settlement relies on a small militia supported by experienced elven hunters and warriors.

## History

Alkai Tor was founded over six centuries ago by nomadic wood elves, who made the granite tor the spiritual centre of their community. [[Elder Rolling-Thunder]] led the village for generations, while his brother, [[Mystic Howling-Gale]], served as its shaman.

Around fifty years before 1486 DR, humans settled nearby. Despite initial suspicion, Rolling-Thunder encouraged peaceful integration, and the mixed community prospered.

In 1486 DR, [[Karahl-Guhl]] and his thralls attacked the village and abducted several residents, including its elder and shaman. The captives were taken to the [[Temple of Shattered Minds]], where the illithid experimented on them. The [[Seekers]] defeated Karahl-Guhl and rescued some prisoners, but Rolling-Thunder died in captivity.

A tribe led by [[Kaghed Togak]] had arrived shortly before the kidnappings seeking a peaceful alliance. Many of its members fell fighting the illithid's forces. Kaghed later aided the Seekers and sacrificed himself during the struggle.

## Notable Residents and Buildings

- **Elder's Longhouse:** Former home of [[Elder Rolling-Thunder]], the village's respected leader.
- **Mystic's Hut:** Home of [[Mystic Howling-Gale]]. After his rescue, he began establishing a mystic order at the [[Temple of Shattered Minds]].
- **Gruzza's Forge:** Run by [[Gruzza]], a reclusive dwarf initially suspected of involvement in the disappearances. The Seekers cleared her of suspicion.
- **Farm:** Home of Hector, a retired adventurer, and Pallas. They lost two children to the thralls.
- **Water Mill:** Home of Siobhan, the miller's daughter and militia captain. She helped maintain order during the crisis and later supported the new mystic order.
- **Brewery:** Run by Alec and Elisse. Elisse was enthralled by Karahl-Guhl and freed after his defeat.

## Surroundings

- **[[Border Mire]]:** Marshland north of the village, fed by two rivers. Natural salt deposits make its eastern reaches a toxic saltwater marsh.
- **[[Temple of Shattered Minds]]:** An ancient ziggurat in the northern marsh, used by [[Karahl-Guhl]] as a prison and laboratory.
- **[[Bog Town]]:** A nearby settlement on the edge of the marsh.
