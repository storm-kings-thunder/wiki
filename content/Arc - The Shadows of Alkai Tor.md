The [[Seekers]] became involved in a series of attacks and disappearances around [[Alkai Tor]] in 1486 DR. The crisis led into the [[Border Mire]], where the illithid [[Karahl-Guhl]] held captives in the [[Temple of Shattered Minds]].

## Major Events

- Karahl-Guhl's campaign followed the death of his companion, [[Zaxab]], during a clash with the village's defenders.
- His thralls abducted residents, including [[Elder Rolling-Thunder]] and [[Mystic Howling-Gale]], for imprisonment and experimentation.
- The Seekers investigated the disappearances, clearing [[Gruzza]] of suspicion, and confronted the illithid.
- [[Kaghed Togak]], whose tribe had sought a peaceful alliance with the village, aided the party and sacrificed himself during the struggle.
- The Seekers defeated Karahl-Guhl and rescued some prisoners, but Rolling-Thunder died in captivity.

## Consequences

The village survived with severe losses, and its population continued to decline. Howling-Gale began establishing a mystic order at the former temple.

Kaghed's sacrifice became a defining moment for [[Ougigoth Togak|Nog]]. [[Kord]] briefly allowed Kaghed's spirit to return and aid the struggle, and his maul, The Last Word, passed to Nog.
