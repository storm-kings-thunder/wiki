In [[Waterdeep]], the [[Seekers]] became entangled in the schemes of the [[Margaster Family]], whose influence had reduced much of the city's nobility to puppets. Public spectacle, espionage, and supernatural bargains lay behind the family's power.

## Major Events

- During the [[Tournament of Waterdeep]] in 1486 DR, [[Simon Sack-of-Potatoes]] sought help after finding a warning that [[Felix]] had to die.
- The party discovered that Felix was a construct built by the [[Hexhammer Clan]], intended to stage a heroic victory for [[Sir Egan]].
- The Margasters' campaign against the Seekers included framing them for the death of a seer possessed by [[Pazuzu]] and infiltrating their lodgings.
- Following [[Leor]]'s death, Margaster spies stole the contract to the [[Extradimensional Mansion]]. The Seekers subsequently reclaimed it.
- The Seekers defeated the family, ending its domination of the city in 1486 DR.

## Consequences

The Margasters lost their control of Waterdeep, and their mansion became city property. The conflict exposed the destructive bargains behind their rise and brought [[Willow Margaster|Willow]]'s family history into the party's wider story.
