A drow mage of the [[Order of the Purple Robes]] specializing in illusion magic.

He was present at the formal dinner in the [[Luminous Cloud Citadel]]. Seems to have a crush on [[Magister Lavinia Brightswan|Lavinia]].

Under a deal brokered through [[Father Rogers]], who appeared in his dreams, Meredin administered a slow poison to [[Eszteban, the Magnificent|Eszteban]] over a long period. Meredin was present in the tower invisibly during the final confrontation, and killed Eszteban by throwing a dagger into his throat. He claimed not to have been sent by [[Magister Lavinia Brightswan|Lavinia]] and appeared to be acting under an external influence rather than purely on his own initiative.

The party took his [[Ring of Invisibility]] and temporarily his spellbook. He was initially left for the Order council to judge; the council subsequently resolved to imprison him.
