It is rumored that this site witnessed the epic battle between the [[Lords of Calimsharn]] and the [[Doom of the Desert]]. The lake itself, a vast expanse of solid glass, stands as the sole remnant of this confrontation, believed to have been created by the dragon's powerful breath. Now, the surface is home to peculiar glass elementals that roam across it, perpetually cycling through a process of shattering and reconstitution.t is rumored that it is the place where the [[Lords of Calimsharn]] fought against the [[Doom of the Desert]]. All that remains of their battle is the lake of glass, a surface entirely made of glass, presumably a consequence from the dragon's breath. Since then, small groups of elementals made of glass travel on it's surface, constantly shattering and reforming.
