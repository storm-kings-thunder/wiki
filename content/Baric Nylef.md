Baric Nylef is an Illuskan human knight of Tyr and a member of the [[Order of the Golden Lion]]. He is a true devotee of Tyr, driven by a desire to cleanse the world of evil while awaiting the day he might meet his god.

In 1485 DR, Baric traveled to [[Bryn Shander]] to capture a notorious criminal rumored to be hiding in the [[Ten Towns]]. He posed as an unemployed caravan guard drinking at [[Kelvin's Comfort]] tavern. He was present at the tavern when giants attacked the city.

*Honorific: Sir. Alignment: Lawful Good.*
