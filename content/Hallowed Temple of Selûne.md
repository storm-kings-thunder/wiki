The Hallowed Temple of Selûne is a temple of [[Selûne, God of the Moon|Selûne]] in [[Neverwinter]]'s [[Blacklake District]]. Its congregation is small, but its clergy are capable of lifting most curses.

In 1486 DR, a ritual was attempted here to remove the [[Kraken]]'s curse afflicting [[Leor]], a member of the [[Seekers]].
