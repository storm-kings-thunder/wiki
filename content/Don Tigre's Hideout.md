Like all secret locations in [[Luskan, the city of Sails|Luskan]], [[Don Tigre]]'s clan hideout is concealed within one of the city's many underground mazes.

Protected by multiple defensive magical runes, the hideout is exceptionally difficult to damage and even harder to find. For many citizens, it is considered a safe haven from the [[Pirate Lords|Council]]-controlled areas of the city, though its size is not sufficient to shelter the entire populace.
