Married to [[Alaeros Margaster]].

Granted immense arcane power by the entity, Xamlyn ruled over the Margaster holdings in [[Waterdeep]] with cold precision. When she and [[Alaeros Margaster|Alaeros]] were cornered during the end of the [[Tournament of Waterdeep]], they attempted to eliminate the city’s remaining leadership. Before they could act, both were decapitated by [[Ougigoth Togak|Nog]].
