A containment vault in [[Illusk]], built to imprison the [[Intelligence from the Stars]], the creature that powers the city's machines. Rebellious officials later sealed [[Hardan Voss]] inside with it. The creature fused with Hardan and began feeding on his consciousness.

The [[Seekers]] found the entrance secured by a runic seal, with a nearby tent and three strokes marked on the wall behind it. After exposure to [[Arcane Radiation|arcane radiation]], [[Red, the Kid|Red]] appeared mentally manipulated into wanting to use its contents to help his clan. He used thieves' tools and a lever at the southern control console to open the gate, releasing Hardan and the creature fused with him. The vault is now open.
