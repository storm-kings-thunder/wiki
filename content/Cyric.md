**Alignment**: Chaotic Evil
**Domains**: Trickery
**Pantheon**: Faerûnian
**Symbol**: White jawless skull on a black or purple sunburst
**Typical Worshipers**: Murderers, traitors, liars, the power-hungry

God of lies, strife, and madness. Cyric was once a mortal man who ascended to godhood in the mid-14th century DR by slaying the god Bhaal at Boareskyr Bridge. He briefly held the portfolios of death, murder, and tyranny before Kelemvor stripped the domain of the dead from him. In 1385 DR, Cyric murdered [[Mystra, God of Magic|Mystra]], triggering the catastrophic Spellplague.

His faith draws the worst of society — unabashed liars, murderers, spies, and traitors. He is a chief enemy of both [[Mystra, God of Magic|Mystra]] and [[Kelemvor, God of the Dead|Kelemvor]].
