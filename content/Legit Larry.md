An eccentric Djinn shopkeeper. In 1486 DR, the [[Seekers]] have visited his shop a few times and have discovered that some of his magical items do not seem to always work as intended.

When a rampaging [[Grothgar]] assaulted his shop, Larry managed to repel him, launching him towards the nearby ocean. Unfortunately, [[Grothgar]] swam back a few hours later.

In the final battle against [[Grothgar]], Larry attempted to intervene and help, but by the time he arrived, [[Grothgar]] had already been killed. In the aftermath, he helped to rebuild the Beached Leviathan with his aerokinetic powers.

Male djinn. Alignment: Chaotic Good.
