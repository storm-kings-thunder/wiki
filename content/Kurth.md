Kurth is one of the surviving [[Pirate Lords]] of [[Luskan, the city of Sails|Luskan]] and a former member of the [[Arcane Brotherhood]]. He has isolated himself in [[Kurth's Tower|his tower]] and is regarded by [[Razzles]] as a perceptive mage.

When the [[Seekers]] came to [[Kurth's Tower|his tower]], Kurth warned them away because he suspected they might be assassins. After they dispelled the magical seal on the door and entered, he transported himself elsewhere.

His guard [[Varlak]] fought the party, who withdrew without defeating him. [[Connor Lycanmor|Connor]], found in the tower under a curse compelling him to attack the Seekers, later used an [[Anchor Stones|Anchor Stone]] to recall Kurth asking him to attack them.

[[Kessara Vold]] said Kurth had contacted one of her superiors. The party understood this as [[Father Rogers]] having contacted that superior, though the precise connection remains uncertain.

During their second assault on the tower, the Seekers found him and cut off the ear bearing [[Kurth's Earring|his magical earring]]. Without it, he was revealed to be a drow, apparently formerly called Beniago. *Identify* showed that the earring gives any wearer Kurth's appearance.
