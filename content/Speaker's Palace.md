The Speaker's Palace is the personal residence of the Speaker of [[Bryn Shander]], the individual who represents the settlement to the rest of the [[Ten Towns]].

The "palace" part of its name is an overstatement compared to such buildings elsewhere in the world. It stands so out of place among the rough wood dwellings of [[Bryn Shander]] that it looks as if it had been magically transported from some other region of Faerûn.

The building is of Dwarven design, constructed with heavy stone walls and a notable colonnade on its facade — the feature most responsible for its incongruous appearance.

Current resident: [[Duvessa Shane]], Speaker of Bryn Shander.
