The Lord of Gold is [[Calimsharn]]'s newest and most secretive Lord, having joined the city about 10 years ago. Little is known about him, except that he is credited with the city's recent economic boom. Many rumors circulate about his identity, but none have been confirmed. 

He was secretly a merchant, and owner of the [[Dusty Talisman]]. He reportedly keeps several debtors turned to gold in his vault.

He sees profit in the possible return of [[Alkhazir]] and has agreed to attend the [[Lord of Knowledge]]'s meeting. He seems to keep several debtors turned to gold in his vault.

He aims to explore a [[Copper Titan]] in the [[Desert]], its arcane core able to turn its copper form into roughly two million gold pieces.

Killed, beaten to death by his devil bodyguard, and later revived by [[Seraphina]].

He gives the [[Seekers]] some favors in return, allowing them to purchase magic items off his store for a discount of 20%.
