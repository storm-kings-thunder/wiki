Thousands of years ago, a powerful undead entity, [[Ulvar]], was captured by the followers of [[Kelemvor, God of the Dead|Kelemvor]] and sealed within a sanctum built specifically to contain it. The legendary archmage [[Ellan Mera]] — once a devoted priestess of [[Kelemvor, God of the Dead|Kelemvor]] and later his apostate — took on the role of the creature’s jailor. She was aided by many faithful servants, among them the renowned swordsman [[Yinsaren]] and a colossal golem of living sandstone.

Unfortunately, the creature’s power was greater than they had imagined. Over the millennia, it spread its corruption throughout the enclave. The waters turned white, saturated with necromantic energy, and a dreadful curse spread among the followers of [[Kelemvor, God of the Dead|Kelemvor]]. A thirst for blood grew within them, and those who succumbed to madness underwent a beastly transformation. 

In the end, only [[Yinsaren]] and [[Ellan Mera]] survived — though both were broken by the curse. Only their shared purpose of keeping the creature imprisoned allowed them to restrain their insanity. Even the guardian golem became corrupted, its body bleeding as if it were flesh.

The [[Seekers]] eventually discovered the long-rumored enclave and, after facing many terrible creatures — including the corrupted golem (with [[Willow Margaster|Willow]] dying in battle against [[Yinsaren]]) — they confronted the corrupted [[Ellan Mera]]. As the [[Seekers]] fought the transformed archmage and her wounds deepened, the seals that kept the creature imprisoned broke, and it was freed. 

Nothing the [[Seekers]] did could stop its escape. With [[Yinsaren]] in its grasp, the entity vanished from the ruins.
