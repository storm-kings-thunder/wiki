A gladiator in the [[Arena of the Desert]]. A strangely friendly Frost Giant who, despite being a Dragon's friend, serves as one of the [[Arena of the Desert]]'s gladiators. He appears to have been an adventurer before joining the arena.

He had an adventuring party of 50, having disbanded more than one decade ago. He wants to solve the Giant Ordning matter as well.
