As his title suggests, the Master of the Arena presides over the [[Arena]]. A silent figure, he maintains an almost statue-like presence in the coliseum, stirring only when combat begins. Rumors suggest that his extensive use of [[Truespeech]] has consumed much of his original personality, which would account for his extraordinary ability to manipulate the arena's terrain at will.

The Master of the Arena has agreed to participate in the meeting called by the [[Lord of Knowledge]].
