Real Name: **Doctor Craven**

![[Lord of Science.png|283x366]]

The Lord of Science's domain is his [[Laboratory]], where he conducts a wide range of experiments assisted by three aides:
1. Dr. Blitznick, a man partially made of glass
2. Dr. Toyo, who is half-mutated
3. Dr. Arnold, who is partially mechanical

Doctor Craven created a vaccine against the [[Red Plague]] and developed an engine capable of extracting the purest essence from the [[Blood Stones]]. Still, he often pursues risky, improvised experiments that sometimes end disastrously — most recently resulting in demon-possessed pigs overrunning his laboratory. Occasionally he supplies the [[Arena]] with some of these "failed" experiments for gladiators to fight.

He harbors a deep disdain for "Superstition," a category that includes anything remotely religious or magical, which has put him at odds with the [[Lord of Knowledge]].

In 1486 DR, he tasked the [[Seekers]] with investigating the [[Tower that found the Truth]] to earn his trust. When they succeeded, he offered them a new commission: to retrieve the [[Emerald Tablet]], once owned by the legendary alchemist [[Alkhazir]].

He joined [[Calimsharn]] roughly 15 years ago.
 
Doctor Craven reports that the vaccine for the [[Red Plague]] has recently stopped working, possibly connected to [[Alkhazir]]'s manifestation. He will attend the [[Lord of Knowledge]]’s meeting, provided the [[Seekers]] help reassure the other [[Lords of Calimsharn]] that the vaccine’s development is progressing well.

Died due to the side effects of a serum injection he used to gain enough power to defeat [[Orthon]].
