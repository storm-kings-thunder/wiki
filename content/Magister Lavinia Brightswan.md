An apprentice of [[Eszteban, the Magnificent|Eszteban]] and a member of the [[Order of the Purple Robes]] focusing on evocation and abjuration. She has slave marks on her neck from the [[Red Wizards of Thay]].

During the [[Luminous Cloud Citadel|Citadel]] crisis, she stated that [[Eszteban, the Magnificent|Eszteban]] had become less sound in both body and mind.

Before the [[Seekers]] entered the tower's upper levels, she contacted [[Maerik 'Andarilho' Brightpeak|Maerik]] telepathically to warn him not to sleep inside the tower.

Her portrait in the Citadel's research room gallery depicts her without the black mask and burns she currently bears, suggesting those injuries occurred after the portrait was made.

[[Eszteban, the Magnificent|Eszteban]] stated that Lavinia attempted to kill him at some point. [[Meredin]], under questioning, confirmed this and noted she would have made a better leader — though he denied acting on her behalf.
