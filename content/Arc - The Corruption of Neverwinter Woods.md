In 1486 DR, [[Archdruid Oloamin]] sent [[Ellora Oak]] and the [[Seekers]] to cleanse a spreading corruption in [[Neverwinter Woods]]. His influence had slowed its advance since 1480 DR, but had not removed its source.

## Major Events

- The party traced the corruption to the [[Monthartas]] family mansion.
- The hag [[Gertrude]] had infiltrated the household decades earlier and manipulated General Monthartas into sacrificing his daughter.
- The sacrifice completed a curse that transformed the estate into a source of necromantic corruption.
- The Seekers killed Gertrude and destroyed the necromantic gem spreading the curse.

## Consequences

Afflicted creatures and trees were purified, or disintegrated where the corruption had taken too deep a hold. In gratitude, [[Silvanus, God of Nature|Silvanus]] transformed Ellora's staff into the [[Heart of the Woodlands]].
