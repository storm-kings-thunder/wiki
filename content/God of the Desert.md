An entity venerated by [[Lyra, the Traveler]], known as "The Waters of Life" and "The Solar Inferno" and "The Unconquerable Sun".

The [[Lord of Knowledge]] shares that they had a strife with [[Alkhazir]] after they gave the mortals the power of [[Alchemy]].

Until recently, it lay in comatose under [[Calimsharn]], where its inhabitants used its blood in the form of [[Blood Stones]]. Now it has begun to manifest by posessing and controlling [[Red Warriors]], using their bodies as vessels to communicate.

It has declared that it will unleash [[Seven Plagues]] in the coming days. The first was an increase in the [[Red Plague]]'s potency. The second was making all [[Red Warriors]] explode, which left the city of [[Calimsharn]] in chaos.

After the [[Red Warriors]]' explosion, the [[Blood Mines Expedition]] resulted in a successful destruction of its heart.
