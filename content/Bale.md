Bale is a middle-aged male dwarf merchant who travels the Sword Coast in a carriage, selling magical items to travellers and occasionally at city auctions.

In 1486 DR, he encountered the [[Seekers]] near [[Port Llast]] and hired them to escort him to an auction in [[Neverwinter]]. During the journey, the group repelled an orc bandit attack. Bale sold several items to the party along the way, including a prismatic axe and an endless mug, and sold off more generic magical items at the auction itself.

Shortly after the [[Seekers]] departed in 1486 DR, Bale continued his travels northeast.

*Alignment: Lawful Good*
