Bale is a middle-aged dwarf merchant who travels the Sword Coast by carriage, selling magic items to travellers and at occasional auctions.

In 1486 DR, Bale met the [[Seekers]] near [[Port Llast]] and hired them to escort him south to [[Neverwinter]] for an auction at the [[Tarmalune Trade House]] in the [[Protector's Enclave]]. The Seekers fought off orc bandits on the road and bought several items from Bale along the way, including a prismatic axe and an endless mug. Bale sold more of his wares at the auction.

Shortly after the Seekers left Neverwinter, Bale resumed his travels, heading northeast.
