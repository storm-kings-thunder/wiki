The Harpers are a secretive network of spies, spellcasters, and adventurers who oppose tyranny and the abuse of power throughout Faerûn. They defend the innocent and vulnerable, promote fairness, and seek to prevent power from becoming concentrated in dangerous hands.

Harpers favor discretion, infiltration, and intelligence gathering over open confrontation. Their agents cultivate informants, adopt cover identities, and share knowledge through trusted contacts. Experienced members can draw on hidden stores of information and sources in towns and cities across the Realms.

[[Artus Cimber]] is a former Harper agent who left after becoming disillusioned with their cautious methods.
