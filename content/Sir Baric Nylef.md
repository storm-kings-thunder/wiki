Baric Nylef is a human knight of [[Tyr]] and a member of the [[Order of the Golden Lion]]. He seeks to rid the world of evil in Tyr's name and hopes to stand before his god one day.

In 1485 DR, Baric travelled to [[Bryn Shander]] to apprehend a notorious dwarf criminal believed to be hiding in the [[Ten Towns]]. Posing as an out-of-work caravan guard, he became a regular at [[Kelvin's Comfort]], hoping his quarry's weakness for Flamebeard's Firebrandy would draw him there. Baric was at the tavern when giants attacked the town.
