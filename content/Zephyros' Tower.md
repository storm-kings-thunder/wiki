The Tower of Zephyros is a floating tower belonging to the cloud giant wizard [[Zephyros]]. It is his house and place of research.

The tower is hundreds of feet tall and rests on a magically solid cloud. The spire is shaped like a pointed wizard's hat.

The interior comprises three stories, each built to gigantic proportions and filled with giant-sized furniture. Each story is connected by a 20-foot-wide hole in the ceiling/floor.

- **First floor:** The giant's sitting room.
- **Second floor:** His bedchambers and the navigation orb that [[Zephyros]] uses to control the tower's movement.
- **Third floor:** An open-air aerie where several griffons make their home.

## History

[[Seekers]] travelled in this tower from [[Nightstone]] to [[Bryn Shander]] (26 days) in 1485 DR. Later in 1486 DR, they also travelled for a few days from [[Neverwinter]] to [[Longsaddle]].

## Denizens

- **The Goblins Tom and Jerry:** During their first mission in Nightstone, the Seekers saved surviving villagers from a goblin tribe. For unknown reasons, they had mercy on two goblins and brought them along for a while. After the trip to Bryn Shander, Tom and Jerry were left in the tower as assistants to [[Zephyros]]. They are getting close to being minimally competent, despite having caused many accidents in their first weeks.
- **[[Nibbles]]:** A hyper-intelligent mutant rat and former companion of Zenvalros, a member of the Seekers. Shortly before reaching Neverwinter, Zenvalros let Nibbles stay in the tower so the rat could realize his dreams of becoming a powerful wizard (and conquering the world).
- **[[Zephyros]]:** The cloud giant himself. Owner of the tower.
