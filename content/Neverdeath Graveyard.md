Consisting of two wide, roughly squared areas of the city, and surrounded by a wall of stone and wood, Neverdeath is the burial grounds of [[Neverwinter]]. It is named from a common blessing given over the dead. As long as the city remained in summer, it is believed that the dead will not turn into undead.

The doomguides from the [[Eternal Order of Kelemvor]] are responsible for protection of this area, but the person mostly responsible for the graveyard is the gravekeeper nicknamed [[Old Morty]] because he has been Neverdeath's gravekeeper for at least a century.

In 1486 DR, the Gnome [[Gnubbar]] was buried in the graveyard by [[Old Morty]] after being killed and brutalized by [[Grothgar]].
