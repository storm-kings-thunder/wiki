A half-elf sent by [[Lord of Science|Doctor Craven]] to investigate the mystery of the [[Tower that found the Truth]]. Her team was massacred and [[Erdanis]] revealed the truth that he saw to [[Valathuriel]], which turned her as mad as him.

Turned herself into the [[Divine Worm]], a cosmic aberration, which fought against the party, assisted by an [[Erdanis]]-possessed [[Connor Lycanmor]].
