Penelope is a powerful Archmage interested in charting the continent and the current head of the Harpell Clan, based in [[Longsaddle]].

She possessed one of the Relics and gave it to the [[Seekers]] after completing her request — an investigation on the Tower of Iron, near the Lost Peaks. Upon receiving confirmation of the extra-terrestrial nature of the mind flayers and the possible location of another astral ship, she gave them her contact in [[Silverymoon]], an eccentric gnome cartographer.

Female human. Alignment: Lawful Neutral.
