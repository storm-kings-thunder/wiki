An evil arcane faction active in northern politics. Their goal is to conduct arcane experiments and accumulate power; they seize control of civilizations to use them as laboratories rather than out of conquest for its own sake.

In recent [[Luskan, the city of Sails|Luskan]] events, they established anti-scrying barriers over the city. Their Tharchion in the city is [[Kessara Vold]], who received the [[Seekers]] at the [[Administrative Tower]]. She justified the occupation as restoring order at the Pirate Lords' request, offered a satisfactory position for [[Don Tigre]]'s clan in exchange for identifying certain people, and explicitly sought [[Krieg]]'s removal as too unstable.

They now control most of [[Luskan, the city of Sails|Luskan]] and appear to be testing a colonial model of control rather than pursuing simple conquest. Their administrative tunnel network has weak points, and their diviners are believed to be based in a central tower.

## Organization

Their forces are highly stratified. Common soldiers are individually capable; knights possess basic magical knowledge. Above them are war mages trained in specialized Thay spellwork, and higher still are specialists and archmages. The organization also fields undead abominations and is governed in part by a council of liches.

[[Zulkir]] is a title of high authority within the faction. [[Kessara Vold]] identified [[Dar'lon Ma]] as the Zulkir who formerly enslaved [[Magister Lavinia Brightswan|Lavinia]].

A lich from this council was responsible for the burns and black mask now worn by [[Magister Lavinia Brightswan|Lavinia]], who bears slave marks from the Red Wizards on her neck.

According to [[Taerl]], the Red Wizards did not come to help Luskan on their own initiative: [[Father Rogers]] brokered contracts after the [[Pirate Lords]] requested aid and brought them into the city.

Visions revealed by [[Connor Lycanmor|Connor]]'s amplified *Identify* showed him restrained among other humanoids while an apparent plague-masked Red Wizard grew and removed a tumour from his body. A different Red Wizard repeatedly whispered commands and placed an effect on him; the purpose of these procedures remains unknown.
