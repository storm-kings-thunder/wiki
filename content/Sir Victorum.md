Sir Victorum is a male human knight of [[Waterdeep]], a member of the [[Order of the Golden Lion]], and a knighted paladin. Alignment: Chaotic Evil.

Born in the streets of [[Waterdeep]] as an impoverished orphan, Sir Victorum always held the ambition of becoming a noble. After years of hard work and combat, he was eventually knighted after a particularly impressive feat of slaying a basilisk single-handedly. During his time as a knight, he grew to become a good friend to [[Morrigan Volderak|Morrigan]], a paladin of the [[Order of the Golden Lion]].

In 1486 DR, he was surprisingly invited to [[Elminster]]'s party in his demiplane. There, he met [[Seekers]] and — after solving the mystery of Elminster's "murder" and nearly being decapitated by [[Connor Lycanmor]] — told them of his desire to root out the corruption of the [[Margaster Family]] in [[Waterdeep]] and suggested finding more allies.

A few days later he helped the group eliminate the blood mage Mortimer and invited his friend [[Morrigan Volderak|Morrigan]] to join the alliance against the Margasters.

Shortly after being sent on a mission to track the Margasters' spy network, he was captured and a clone of him was used to trick Solomon into being mentally controlled by one of the spies for a day. When that failed, the Margasters decapitated the clone and sent its head to [[Seekers]] to frighten them into leaving [[Waterdeep]].

He was eventually rescued by the party and later assisted the group during the Black Talon's attack on the tavern and in the final battle against the Margasters.

As of now, he is currently being taught proper social etiquette in preparation to replace the Margasters' seat in the council of noble lords.
