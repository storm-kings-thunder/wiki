A bald [[Red Wizards of Thay|Red Wizard]] and the Tharchion administering the occupation of [[Luskan, the city of Sails|Luskan]]. She received the [[Seekers]] in the third-floor meeting room of the [[Administrative Tower]], after inviting them by a letter addressed to the visitors from the Nether Mountains and bearing their names. An unnamed, lower-class human messenger, approximately eighteen years old, delivered it on their way to [[Kurth's Tower]].

Kessara presents the occupation as efficient government restoring order and civilization after the conflict between [[Don Tigre]]'s clan and the [[Pirate Lords]]. She claims the Pirate Lords all but begged for Thayan aid, and views Luskan as a useful point of contact for Thay in exchange for establishing order. She offered the clan a satisfactory position if the Seekers identified certain people for her; the notes do not specify whom. She explicitly wants [[Krieg]] removed from the equation as too unstable.

She claimed [[Magister Lavinia Brightswan|Lavinia]] had been the property of [[Zulkir]] [[Dar'lon Ma]] and escaped a heavily guarded fortress. Her warning that the Zulkir has a long memory implied a potential conflict with Lavinia's allies. She also said [[Kurth]] had contacted one of her superiors; the party understood this to mean [[Father Rogers]] had contacted that superior, though his precise role remains uncertain.
