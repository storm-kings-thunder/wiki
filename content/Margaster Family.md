[[Xamlyn Margaster|Xamlyn]] and [[Alaeros Margaster|Alaeros]] were once a young, impoverished couple in [[Waterdeep]], serving a corrupt and abusive noble lord. One fateful night, an unknown entity approached them with a dreadful bargain: wealth and power in exchange for the noble’s death.

They accepted, the murder was committed, and from that moment began the Margaster family’s descent into evil.

As their influence in [[Waterdeep]] grew, so too did their greed and thirst for power. In time, the same entity returned with another offer — to sacrifice their own children in exchange for dominion through the strength of ancient spirits known as the [[Four Kings]].

Though unwilling to kill them all, [[Xamlyn Margaster|Xamlyn]] and [[Alaeros Margaster|Alaeros]] convinced themselves to make a “compromise”: every third child born would be offered as a sacrifice.
