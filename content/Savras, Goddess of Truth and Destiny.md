**Alignment**: Lawful Neutral
**Category**: None
**Domains**: Arcana, Knowledge
**Pantheon**: Faerûnian
**Province**: Divination, fortunetelling, truth, fate
**Symbol**: Crystal ball containing many kinds of eyes

A god of divination and fortunetelling. Few people worship Savras, but many pray to him when performing small rituals of foresight — for example, young men and women sometimes attempt to divine the names of their future spouses by saying a rhyming chant that calls upon Savras while gazing in a mirror.

Savras has no currently active temples in Faerûn, and his shrines are few and far between, tucked into corners of libraries and scriptoria. His devout worshippers include investigators, diviners, judges, and others who need to uncover the truth, often identifiable by the elaborate staffs they carry in his homage. According to legend, Savras was trapped in Azuth's staff for ages; Azuth eventually freed him in exchange for a sworn fealty, making the staff a potent symbol for his faithful.

Revered by agents such as [[Inquisitor Valerius]].
