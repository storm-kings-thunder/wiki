God of Strength, Battle, and Athletics. Kord grants power to warriors who prove themselves through honorable combat.

In 1486 DR, when the orc chieftain Kaghed Togak was slain near [[Alkai Tor]], Kord briefly granted his spirit a temporary body so that he could make amends with his son [[Ougigoth Togak|Nog Buzzkiller]] and aid the fight against the illithid [[Karahl-Guhl]]. Kaghed ultimately sacrificed himself and passed his legendary maul, "The Last Word," to Nog.
