**Alignment**: Lawful Good
**Domains**: Life
**Pantheon**: Faerûnian
**Symbol**: Pair of white hands bound at the wrist with a red cord
**Typical Worshipers**: Healers, the oppressed, the suffering, martyrs

God of endurance, martyrdom, perseverance, and compassion. Ilmater is the patron of those who suffer unjustly — the poor, the tortured, and the downtrodden. His clergy are known for self-sacrifice in service to others, refusing to abandon the suffering even at cost to themselves.

Ilmater is one of the three gods of the Triad, alongside [[Tyr]] and [[Torm]], all honored in the [[House of the Triad]] in [[Bryn Shander]].
