Member of the [[Seekers]], afflicted with [[Lycantrophy|lycanthropy]]. He recognizes it as a disease but does not necessarily want it removed.

During the [[Desert]] episode, he was teleported into a forest near [[Neverwinter]]. After asking for directions in a village, he followed the trail to [[Luskan, the city of Sails|Luskan]], knowing [[Red, the Kid|Red]] came from there.

The party found him in [[Kurth's Tower]], where he attacked in bear form under a curse. Recognizing [[Red, the Kid|Red]] and [[Zenvalros]] broke the curse compelling him to attack; he offered to help and mentioned an unidentified female figure. Using an [[Anchor Stones|Anchor Stone]], he saw himself speaking with [[Kurth]], who asked him to attack the party in the tower. The Seekers withdrew without defeating Kurth's guard [[Varlak]], then returned to [[Don Tigre's Hideout]].

An amplified *Identify* cast on himself revealed visions of his past: he was restrained in a room with other similarly positioned humanoids, while a figure in Red Wizard robes, apparently wearing a plague doctor's mask, approached him. In pain and growling, Connor tried to bite the figure; a tumour grew on him and was removed once it had grown. In another vision, a different [[Red Wizards of Thay|Red Wizard]] repeatedly whispered commands and placed an effect on his body. The precise nature of these procedures remains unknown.
