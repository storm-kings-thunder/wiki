Darvan is a ghost in [[Illusk]] who draws maps of the city, apparently to track yearly changes to its layout. He asked whether the [[Seekers]] came from the surface and whether the survivors had rebuilt the old empire's colonies. He described the original inhabitants as dead and the current occupants as "guests", presumed by the party to be drow.

He was surprised that the old empire had not been completely destroyed. He says the survivors were forced into the depths and await rescue from what remains of the surface.

He asked whether [[Hardan Voss]] had contacted the party and warned them not to follow that creature. According to Darvan, Hardan was the only official with administrative powers when the Cataclysm occurred. He hired The Stranger, a human in red robes, as an adviser and assistant. Under Hardan's tyranny, evacuation routes were falsified to drive people considered a waste of resources out of the shelters. Some officials rebelled and sealed him in [[Illusk's Vault|the vault]]; the party later encountered him in a new form capable of opening fractures in reality.

Darvan says an [[Intelligence from the Stars|intelligence from the stars]] is contained in the vault and must not escape. The drow apparently respect the site and use an energy source below to keep it active. They benefit from the intelligence and may feel responsible for keeping the vault sealed. He believes its containment may be why they are still here.

Darvan says his own actions keep him bound to Illusk; he apparently killed many people. He described the empire's collapse as beginning in the sky, with many flying cities falling and Illusk nearly sinking into the depths of the earth.
