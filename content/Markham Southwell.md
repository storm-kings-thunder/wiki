Markham Southwell is the sheriff of [[Bryn Shander]].

In 1485 DR, he was informed of his sister's death by goblins, which occurred in [[Nightstone]], by the [[Seekers]]. As thanks, he rewarded them with a sizeable amount of gold pieces. Later, he was at the town hall when the giants attacked.

A man of few words with the mission to protect his citizens. He judged people only by their actions and never showed his emotions.

Human Turami male. Alignment: Lawful Good.
