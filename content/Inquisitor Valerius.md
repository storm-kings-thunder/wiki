A detective of the [[Lords Alliance]], associated with the faith of [[Savras, God of Divination and Fate]]. Serious and methodical. He is the leader of the [[Quake Breakers]].

He briefed the [[Seekers]] on a tsunami that struck nearly every coastal settlement on the Sword Coast, with the epicenter placed west of Waterdeep. He believes the tsunami and a wave of coordinated pirate attacks are connected — both symptoms of a maritime enemy that is organized, possesses magic, and has significant resources and reach. Cnidaran assassins attempted to kill him; he survived by setting a trap.

He noted that Waterdeep, Neverwinter, and Baldur's Gate are all focused inward (political consolidation, guild strikes, and martial law respectively), leaving the sea threat unopposed. [[Gundarlun]], a [[Lords Alliance]] island, is the only force actively engaging pirates at sea. He has no intelligence on [[Luskan, the city of Sails|Luskan]] due to anti-scrying wards. The [[Red Wizards of Thay]] sent a Tarkion to Luskan. He sees no direct link between the collapse of the Giant Ordning and the [[Kraken]] threat, but considers the timing suspicious.

He gave the party the [[Lords' Alliance Sending Stone]] — a badge of the Alliance that opens certain doors and allows one Sending per day to reach him.

[[Seraphina]] initially sought an audience with him regarding the [[Kraken Society]].
