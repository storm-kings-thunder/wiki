The fifth child of the [[Margaster Family]], Brunyund became a formidable arcane warrior who made a pact with a fiendish entity to further his power. Despite his corruption, he cared deeply for his brother [[Willow Margaster|Willow]] and even aided the [[Seekers]] during their stay in [[Waterdeep]], attempting to dissuade them from their mission against his family. 

However, when the [[Black Talon]] assaulted the tavern where the group stayed, Brunyund betrayed [[Willow Margaster|Willow]] and was slain for it. His death served as the third sacrifice required to summon the [[Four Kings]].
