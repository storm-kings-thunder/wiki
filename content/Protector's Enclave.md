The Protector's Enclave is the seat of government and main market district of [[Neverwinter]]. It takes its name from [[Dagult Neverember]], the city's Lord Protector. Many of its buildings survived the eruption of [[Mount Hotenow]], preserving much of the city's pre-eruption appearance.

## History

When Mount Hotenow erupted in 1451 DR, the pyroclastic flow largely spared southwestern Neverwinter. Neverember began rebuilding there and merged the former City Core, Peninsula District, and Merchant Quarter into a single district. Survivors and newcomers repopulated the area, and many became staunch supporters of his rule. The district became known as the [[Protector's Enclave]] after Neverember established his seat of power there.

## Commerce

The city's main market stands in the Enclave. Merchants from [[Waterdeep]] and other Sword Coast cities pay steep taxes to sell their wares there.

## Notable locations

- **[[Hall of Justice]].** Neverwinter's temple of [[Tyr]], overseen by [[High Judge Olsaf]], a stoic follower of the god. During the rebuilding of the late 1470s DR, the temple was incorporated into Neverember's palace to help secure the seat of government. Built of stone, iron, and wood, it can accommodate giants, and its great hall is large enough for dragons to rest in.
- **[[Tarmalune Trade House]].** An open-air auction post frequented by adventurers. In 1486 DR, the [[Seekers]] escorted [[Bale]] to an auction here, where he sold magic items and the party bought a pyramid containing a treasure map.
- **[[House of Knowledge]].** A temple of [[Oghma, God of Knowledge]] and home to Neverwinter's largest library. Badly damaged in the eruption, the tall, many-windowed building was restored in 1484 DR.
- **Five Spires.** A floating complex of five connected towers that houses a monastic order led by [[Grandmaster Amon|Grandmaster Amon]]. Its monks follow the Way of the Long Death, and the order maintains a military alliance with Neverwinter. Visitors arrive at the reception spire. The other four house the monks, a training ground, a hall, and the masters' quarters.
