Former pirate captain and [[Leor]]'s crewmate. Now a member of the [[Kraken Society]]. Owner of the [[Beached Leviathan]], a popular inn and tavern in [[Neverwinter]].

During his youth, Harrag was an infamous pirate feared along the Sword Coast. For him, no ship was too formidable a target and no treasure too small. His days as a pirate came to an end when, at some point before 1479 DR, the Leviathan ran aground during a storm before the Docks of Neverwinter had been rebuilt. Unable to sail ever again, Harrag refurbished the Leviathan into a tavern — the Beached Leviathan — which soon became popular because of its shape. He opened his establishment thanks to the patronage of [[Dagult Neverember]], who also cleared Harrag's past records in exchange for his support to the New Neverwinter movement.

During the [[Seekers]]' visit to [[Neverwinter]] in 1486 DR, Harrag hosted the group and they participated in a gambling tournament held in the Beached Leviathan, whose winner was strangely Harrag himself. During [[Grothgar]]'s rampage, his tavern was assaulted in an attempt to kill off the group. Together with the [[Seekers]], Harrag managed to repel the Orc despite heavy damage to the tavern. He later hosted a small celebration for the group, together with [[Legit Larry]] and [[Gnobbar]].

Male dwarf. Alignment: Chaotic Neutral.
