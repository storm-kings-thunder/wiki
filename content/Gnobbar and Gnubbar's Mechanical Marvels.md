Gnobbar and Gnubbar's Mechanical Marvels is a ramshackle workshop in [[Neverwinter]]'s [[Blacklake District]], founded by the eccentric gnome twins [[Gnobbar]] and [[Gnubbar]] to test and sell their inventions.

In 1486 DR, the [[Seekers]] purchased a chain mace, a repeater crossbow, and a spy fly here. Gnobbar also lent them a powerful weapon for the [[Arena of the Desert]] in exchange for promoting the shop. The party later attempted to steal it, though Gnobbar eventually forgave them.

During [[Grothgar]]'s rampage, he attacked the workshop, killing Gnubbar and severely wounding Gnobbar. The Seekers took Gnubbar's body to the [[Neverdeath Graveyard]] for burial. Gnobbar later helped them fight Grothgar with a newly built long-range firearm.
