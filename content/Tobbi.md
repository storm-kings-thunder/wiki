Tobbi is a young farmer boy with grandiose ambitions to become an adventurer. In 1486 DR, he helped a Modron "named" [[312]] in hopes of leaving with him to become an adventurer. After the [[Seekers]] helped him and [[312]], he changed his mind on becoming an adventurer and decided to help his family's farm, at least for a few years.

Male Illuskan human. Alignment: Neutral Good. Based in [[Thornwood]].
