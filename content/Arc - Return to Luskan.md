The active [[Seekers]] returned to [[Luskan, the city of Sails|Luskan]] to find its balance of power transformed by the [[Red Wizards of Thay]]. Their effort to reunite [[Red, the Kid|Red]]'s divided family led through negotiations, a rescue, and the ruins beneath the city.

## Major Events

- The party negotiated a naval and commercial agreement with [[Taerl]].
- In [[Kurth's Tower]], they reunited with [[Connor Lycanmor|Connor]] and broke the curse compelling him to attack them.
- [[Kessara Vold]] offered terms for [[Don Tigre]]'s clan and sought [[Krieg]]'s removal. No acceptance of her proposal is recorded.
- Returning to Kurth's Tower, the Seekers seized [[Kurth's Earring]], revealing him as a drow apparently formerly called Beniago.
- They descended into [[Illusk]], encountered [[Darvan]], and learned of a drow group whose unnamed leader wears a feathered hat.
- After exposure to [[Arcane Radiation]], Red appeared to be mentally influenced into opening [[Illusk's Vault]]. [[Hardan Voss]] emerged in a form capable of opening fractures in reality.

## Consequences

Connor rejoined the active party. The release of Hardan Voss introduced a new threat amid the struggle for control of Luskan.

## Unresolved Threads

The arc remains ongoing. The Seekers have yet to resolve the family's divisions, determine their response to the Thayan occupation, or address the consequences of opening the vault.
