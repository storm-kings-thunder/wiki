Real name: **Travis**
Member of the [[Seekers]], part of the [[Luskan, the city of Sails|Luskan]] mafia and descendant of the archdevil [[Father Rogers]].

At some point, [[Razzles]] stated that Red's parents had been cultists of [[Father Rogers]].

[[Don Tigre]] later revealed that [[Red, the Kid|Red]] was meant to be a sacrifice before Tigre saved him, and suspects Red descends from a powerful fiend of the [[Nine Hells]]. Red has asked [[Razzles]] for help freeing himself from [[Father Rogers]]' contract and left [[Rogers' Scimitar]] with him.

Red negotiated a partnership with [[Taerl]], who offered Don Tigre's clan four ships and temporary crews in exchange for access to business opportunities in the [[Black Market]]. When [[Father Rogers]] urged him to seize command of the [[Pirate Lords]] and impose order on Luskan through any means necessary, Red rejected Rogers' violent vision.

Red is interested in learning more about [[Kelemvor, God of the Dead|Kelemvor]] and possibly joining his faithful because the faith promotes balance and order. He may seek guidance from [[Seraphina]] in the future.

While investigating energy in a pit in [[Illusk]], Red gained one level of [[Arcane Radiation|arcane radiation]] exposure. [[Maerik 'Andarilho' Brightpeak|Maerik]] noticed his desire to open [[Illusk's Vault]] and use its energy or contents to help his clan, suspecting mental manipulation related to the exposure. Under this influence, Red used thieves' tools and a lever at the southern control console to open the gate, releasing [[Hardan Voss]], now a creature capable of opening fractures in reality.
