Real name: **Travis**
Member of the [[Seekers]], part of the [[Luskan, the city of Sails|Luskan]] mafia and descendant of the archdevil [[Father Rogers]].

At some point, [[Razzles]] stated that Red's parents had been cultists of [[Father Rogers]].

[[Don Tigre]] later revealed that [[Red, the Kid|Red]] was meant to be a sacrifice before Tigre saved him, and suspects Red descends from a powerful fiend of the [[Nine Hells]]. Red has asked [[Razzles]] for help freeing himself from [[Father Rogers]]' contract and left [[Rogers' Scimitar]] with him.
