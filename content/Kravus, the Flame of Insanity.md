A volatile gladiator, Kravus is rumored to be either a mad dwarf from [[Mithral Hall]] or a former assistant of [[Lord of Science|Doctor Craven]], deemed too unstable for the doctor's standards. His pyromania manifests in spectacular explosive displays during arena combat.

Died on the [[Blood Mines Expedition]] by suicide bombing to seal a tunnel.
