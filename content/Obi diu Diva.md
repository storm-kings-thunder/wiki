An eccentric genie from the Plane of Fire, bound by a decade-long contract to [[Eszteban, the Magnificent|Eszteban]] to serve as a guardian in the upper levels of the [[Luminous Cloud Citadel]].

He presented himself as a chess grandmaster and offered any intruders a choice between a drinking challenge or a chess match. Visibly bored with his post and indifferent to the nature of the work, he appeared to view the contract as a tedious obligation rather than a vocation.

When the [[Seekers]] encountered him, they used Banishment to send him back to his plane rather than accepting either challenge.
