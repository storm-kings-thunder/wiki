Archibald is a middle-aged merchant and noble lord of [[Waterdeep]]. He amassed his fortune by sacrificing his firstborn's soul to [[Pazuzu]], later purchasing his noble title with the wealth gained. Unexpectedly, he fathered his firstborn child a few years after making the pact. Over the next decade and a half, he spent a considerable sum attempting to break the deal with the demon prince.

In 1486 DR, his son was killed as part of the machinations of the [[Margaster Family]] and [[Mortimer]]. Driven by revenge, Archibald joined the [[Seekers]], [[Nabulee el Jasafir]], and [[Sir Victorum]] in their alliance against the [[Margaster Family|Margasters]], assisting in the final battle against them.

When the [[Seekers]] learned of his pact with [[Pazuzu]], [[Connor Lycanmor]] issued an ultimatum: Archibald would leave the city with his fortune, and in return, the party would save his son's soul. Archibald reluctantly accepted and is now apparently preparing to move to [[Mithral Hall]].
