Taerl is one of the surviving [[Pirate Lords]] of [[Luskan, the city of Sails|Luskan]], known as a merchant and diplomat. [[Don Tigre]] believes Taerl is acting behind the backs of the [[Red Wizards of Thay]].

Taerl claims that [[Father Rogers]] brokered contracts through which the Pirate Lords requested aid and brought the Red Wizards to Luskan. He wants Rogers, the Thayans, and the other outsiders removed, but expects a seat on the resulting council and a share of their wealth and ships.

After weeks of negotiations, Taerl and [[Don Tigre]] had nearly secured an alliance when [[Krieg]] launched an ambush against the Pirate Lords. Taerl later reached a separate agreement with [[Red, the Kid|Red]]: he offered Don Tigre's clan four ships with temporary crews in exchange for a partnership that would bring some of his business into the [[Black Market]]. Although he tested the party by serving wine laced with a paralytic, he provided the antidote once the meeting ended well and appeared opportunistic rather than treacherous.
