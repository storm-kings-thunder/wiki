Domain of the [[Lord of Knowledge]] in [[Calimsharn]]. 

It is large territory composed of 3 buildings and an auditorium. Two of the buildings are dedicated to teaching classes, while the third holds a library and the office of the [[Lord of Knowledge]].

A misplaced document found in the student section of the library revealed correspondence between [[Eszteban, the Magnificent]] and [[Lord of Knowledge|Professor Ishmael]] regarding the [[Discoveries]] project and the creation of a curated archive of spells.
