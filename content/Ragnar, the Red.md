

Ragnar was born into the [[Griffon Tribe]] in 1450 DR and trained as a Totem Warrior by its warrior-shamans. [[Kralgar Bonesnapper]] took him under his wing and groomed him to succeed him as chief. Ragnar helped raise the Griffon Tribe to prominence and believed the blood he had shed had served that purpose.

When Kralgar revealed his ambition to conquer a city on the Sword Coast, Ragnar opposed him. Kralgar raised the [[Great Barbarian Horde]] anyway and marched on [[Silverymoon]] in 1470 DR. Knocked unconscious during the [[Battle for Silverymoon]], Ragnar woke among hundreds of dead. Despite his wounds, he walked for miles before collapsing again. Slavers from [[Luskan, the city of Sails|Luskan]] found him and took him captive.

Ragnar became [[Champion of the Pit]], earning the name Ragnar the Red. In 1486 DR, [[Zenvalros]] became the second fighter to defeat him and used his winnings to buy Ragnar's freedom. Ragnar now travels the Sword Coast, seeking to reunite the barbarian tribes and free them from leaders like Kralgar.
