An eccentric Gnome engineer who co-founded a shop selling mechanical items in [[Neverwinter]] with his twin brother [[Gnubbar]].

During the [[Seekers]]' visit to [[Neverwinter]] in 1486 DR, Gnobbar lent them a powerful weapon for use in the [[Arena]] in return for the group sponsoring his shop. When the group attempted to steal this weapon, he eventually forgave them.

A few days later, his brother [[Gnubbar]] was killed when [[Grothgar]] attacked their shop, leaving Gnobbar also heavily wounded. In the final showdown against [[Grothgar]], Gnobbar intervened in the [[Seekers]]' favor with his newly created long-range firearm.

When a malfunctioning magical tower threatened to destroy [[Neverwinter]], Gnobbar assisted the group in their mission, using a device he created to track how much time they had left until the tower crashed over the city.

Gnome male. Alignment: Chaotic Good.
