A legendary alchemical artifact of immense power and renown. Said to be capable of transmuting base metals into gold and producing the Elixir of Life.

Though celebrated as one of the greatest alchemical achievements in history, it is surpassed in significance by the [[Emerald Tablet]] — the foundational work of [[Alkhazir]], the very inventor of [[Alchemy]].
