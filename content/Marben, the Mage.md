A formidable wizard who has honed both magical and physical prowess for [[Arena]] combat. As the longest-serving active gladiator with over a decade of fights, he had long abandoned hope of freedom. However, [[Red, the Kid|Red]]'s recent suggestion of rebellion has rekindled his desire for liberation.

Killed by the [[Infernal Knight]]
