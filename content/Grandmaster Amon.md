Amon is the former grandmaster of the Order of the Five Spires, a monastic order based in the [[Protector's Enclave]] of [[Neverwinter]]. The order follows the Way of the Long Death. [[Zenvalros]] trained under Amon, left the order over a disagreement with its ideals, and later fought him.

Amon visited the monastery where [[Shan]] trains and asked what happens when a mental connection is forced. He left abruptly, saying he needed to go to the mountains. Shan recalls that he seemed determined to turn himself into a tool, whatever the cost. His current whereabouts are unknown.
