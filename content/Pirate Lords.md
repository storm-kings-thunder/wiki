A coalition of sea captains whose fleets recently became the dominant naval force around [[Luskan, the city of Sails|Luskan]].

One of their captains was killed by [[Zarobataal]], triggering instability and retaliation cycles in the region.

The council has been badly weakened. Of the known Pirate Lords, one was devoured by a demonic shark, another was killed in an ambush by [[Krieg]], and the main survivors are [[Taerl]], [[Baram]], and [[Kurth]].

According to Taerl, the council requested outside aid and [[Father Rogers]] brokered the contracts that brought the [[Red Wizards of Thay]] into Luskan. Taerl now wants both Rogers and the Thayans removed and seeks a seat on whatever council replaces them.

## Members

- [[Taerl]]: merchant and diplomat.
- [[Baram]]: military power in [[Luskan, the city of Sails|Luskan]], now withdrawn into his fortress.
- [[Kurth]]: former [[Arcane Brotherhood]] mage isolated in his tower.
