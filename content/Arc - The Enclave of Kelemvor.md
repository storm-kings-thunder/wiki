The [[Seekers]] discovered the [[Enclave of Kelemvor]], an ancient sanctuary built to imprison [[Ulvar]]. Over millennia, its prison had become a place of necromantic corruption.

## Major Events

- The party encountered the remains of a community whose guardians had succumbed to a bloodthirsty curse.
- They faced a corrupted sandstone golem and the swordsman [[Yinsaren]], who killed [[Willow Margaster|Willow]] in battle.
- They confronted [[Ellan Mera]], the archmage and former priestess charged with guarding Ulvar.
- As Ellan Mera was wounded in the confrontation, the seals containing the prisoner broke.
- Ulvar escaped with Yinsaren in its grasp, beyond the party's ability to stop it.

## Consequences

The enclave failed in its ancient purpose, and Ulvar was freed. Willow's death marked a major loss during the expedition.

The escaped entity's subsequent fate remains an unresolved threat.
