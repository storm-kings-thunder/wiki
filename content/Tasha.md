Tasha was a jester possessed by a demon that preyed on entire settlements. For decades, the entity travelled between villages, luring their inhabitants into a twisted carnival dimension where victims were transformed into pastries and their souls devoured.

After claiming [[Parnast]], Tasha targeted [[Amphail]] in 1486 DR and drew its inhabitants into her carnival. The [[Seekers]] arrived a week later and pursued her through the dimension's horrors, ultimately killing her.

Her death freed thousands of captives who had not yet been consumed. With the Seekers' guidance, they settled in Amphail and began building a new community.
