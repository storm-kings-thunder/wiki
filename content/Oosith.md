Oosith was an aboleth lieutenant of the [[Kraken]]'s cult in [[Yartar]], where he helped coordinate the kidnapping of multiple nobles with the aid of [[Lady Atalia]].

When [[Lady Atalia]] attempted to redirect the [[Seekers]] into killing Oosith to replace him as lieutenant, he anticipated her betrayal. Disguised as a human wizard, he revealed her treachery to the group, then emerged in his true form and triggered a deadly underwater battle. He was killed by former Seeker [[Korosu]]. [[Red, the Kid|Red]] took one of his teeth as a trophy, but it disintegrated within a month.
