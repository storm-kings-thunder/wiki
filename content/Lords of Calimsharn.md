The founding council of [[Calimsharn]] and its ruling body. The original four founding members — the [[Lady of Blood]], the [[Lord of Knowledge]], the [[Lord of Slaves]], and the [[Master of the Arena]] — were once adventurers who attempted to reclaim the [[Desert]] from the ancient dragon known as the [[Doom of the Desert]] in 1436 DR. Though they did not defeat the dragon outright, they wounded it enough that it retreated to its lair and ceased interfering with the city.

In 1471 DR, the [[Lord of Science]] joined the council after playing a crucial role in developing a remedy for the [[Red Plague]]. In 1476 DR, the [[Lord of Gold]], the most recent addition, joined after significantly improving the city's economy.

Each Lord governs a specific domain within the city and generally refrains from interfering in the responsibilities of others. However, a majority decision among the Lords can override an individual Lord's actions.

[[Lady of Blood]] - Kareem el Mustar: The Lady of Blood oversees the hospital and part of the mines. In the [[Desert]], where divine power is scarce, she treats miners afflicted by the [[Red Plague]] and removes mutations with the aid of the [[Lord of Science]]'s vaccine and her biological [[Truespeech]]. She guards her position as the only accomplished healer in [[Calimsharn]]. In 1486 DR, she hired the [[Seekers]] to track down a group of escaped patients infected with an unusual strain of [[Vampirism]] who had massacred others in the hospital. Her bodyguard and "son," [[Ankhor]], is a massive humanoid with a childlike mind but surprisingly advanced vocabulary.

[[Master of the Arena]] - true name unknown: The Master of the Arena presides over the [[Arena]] and is a largely silent figure who remains motionless until combat begins. His extensive use of [[Truespeech]] appears to have consumed much of his former self, explaining his uncanny ability to reshape the arena's terrain.

[[Lord of Slaves]] - Taalib el Hakim: The Lord of Slaves commands his [[Palace]] and many slaves, and has cultivated fiendish allies through his Truespeech. He is known for granting wishes to those who fulfill their contracts, a power he demonstrated when he aided the [[Seekers]] in the effort to free [[Felguros]].

[[Lord of Science]] - Doctor Craven: The Lord of Science operates his [[Laboratory]] with the assistance of three unusual aides: Dr. Blitznick (partially made of glass), Dr. Toyo (half-mutated), and Dr. Arnold (partially mechanical). He developed a vaccine against the [[Red Plague]] and an engine to extract the essence from [[Blood Stones]], though some of his experiments have had chaotic results. He deeply distrusts "Superstition," which has created friction with the [[Lord of Knowledge]].

[[Lord of Knowledge]] - Professor Ishmael: The Lord of Knowledge runs the [[University]], where he trains students — and selects apprentices for [[Truespeech]]. His research focuses on the origins and mechanics of Truespeech and on recovering lost lore such as the [[Emerald Tablet]].

[[Lord of Gold]] - unknown: The Lord of Gold is [[Calimsharn]]'s most secretive Lord. Little is known about him beyond his role in revitalizing the city's economy; rumors suggest he was formerly a merchant.

It appears the founding [[Lords of Calimsharn]] forged a pact with [[Father Rogers]], one that dooms them to lose their souls should they ever break it. That pact is likely related to their [[Truespeech]] powers.
