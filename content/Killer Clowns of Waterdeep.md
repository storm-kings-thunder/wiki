The Killer Clowns of Waterdeep were assassins from [[Don Tigre]]'s clan in [[Luskan, the city of Sails|Luskan]] who posed as a travelling circus.

In 1486 DR, the troupe used its act at the [[Harvest Festival]] in [[Neverwinter]]'s [[Docks District]] as cover for an attempt to kill [[Baron Rikkler]], a visiting noble from [[Bryn Shander]]. The killing was meant to provoke a war between the two cities. [[Razzles]] warned the [[Seekers]], who foiled the attempt and defeated the assassins. It remains unknown who gave the order.
