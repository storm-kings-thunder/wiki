
A clandestine group of assassins from [[Don Tigre]]'s clan in [[Luskan, the city of Sails|Luskan]] who earned their name by disguising themselves as a travelling circus. 

In 1486 DR, they were ordered by an unknown higher up to assassinate [[Baron Rikkler]], thus provoking a war between [[Bryn Shander]] and [[Neverwinter]]. Thankfully, their assassination failed and they were eliminated by the [[Seekers]].
 
## Members

- Andrano, the Arrow Catcher
- Tarthus, Magore, and Orlock, the Acrobat Dwarves
- Gax, the Gladiator - revealed to be the temporary alias used by [[Grothgar]]
- Bogo, Izalitore, Brythe and Vantzy, the Killer Clowns
