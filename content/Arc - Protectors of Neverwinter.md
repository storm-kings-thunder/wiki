During their time in [[Neverwinter]] in 1486 DR, the [[Seekers]] intervened in several threats to the city. These events brought them recognition as the Protectors of Neverwinter.

## Major Events

- The party became involved in a plot to assassinate [[Baron Rikkler]], a visitor from [[Bryn Shander]].
- [[Razzles]] warned that the assassination, planned within [[Don Tigre]]'s clan, could provoke war between Neverwinter and Bryn Shander.
- The Seekers also intervened in the crisis surrounding [[Grothgar]] and the massacre he caused.
- A malfunctioning flying mage tower nearly destroyed the city; the party helped avert the disaster.

## Consequences

The Seekers helped protect the city from political and magical catastrophes and earned their honorary title.

Their adventures in the nearby forest form a separate episode, [[Arc - The Corruption of Neverwinter Woods]].
