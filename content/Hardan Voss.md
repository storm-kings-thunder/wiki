An official who ruled [[Illusk]] after the Cataclysm and, according to [[Darvan]], took advice from a human in red robes known as The Stranger. According to [[Darvan]], his rule became tyrannical and rebellious officials sealed him in [[Illusk's Vault]].

[[Red, the Kid|Red]], apparently under mental influence after exposure to [[Arcane Radiation|arcane radiation]], opened the vault through the southern control console. Hardan emerged in a new form: a creature capable of opening fractures in reality.
