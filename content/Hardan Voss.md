Hardan Voss was an official who ruled [[Illusk]] after the Cataclysm, advised by [[Father Rogers|The Stranger]]. According to [[Darvan]], his rule became tyrannical, and his officials rebelled and sealed him inside [[Illusk's Vault]].

The vault already contained the [[Intelligence from the Stars]], a creature whose energy powers Illusk's machines. Rather than killing Hardan, it fused with him and fed on his consciousness.

[[Red, the Kid|Red]], apparently under mental influence after exposure to [[Arcane Radiation|arcane radiation]], opened the vault through the southern control console. Hardan emerged fused with the creature and capable of opening fractures in reality.
