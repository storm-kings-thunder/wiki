A human employee aboard the ship-casino [[Golden Goose]]. In secret, she was an agent of the [[Kraken Society]], using seduction to manipulate various nobles and aid [[Oosith]] in their kidnappings. When the visiting [[Seekers]] grew suspicious, she tried to deceive them into killing [[Oosith]], portraying herself as an innocent victim. Her scheme failed, and before she could expose any secrets, the [[Kraken]] killed her telepathically.

Ultimately, she was used as a scapegoat by [[Lord Akain]].
