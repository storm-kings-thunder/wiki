Outwardly, Mortimer was an elderly adventurer who supplied magic items to the Bazaar of [[Nabulee el Jasafir]]. Secretly, however, he was a powerful blood mage from the [[Orient]] who arrived in [[Waterdeep]] with the objective of opening a portal into the [[Shadowlands]], and thus corrupting the entirety of the city. It was later also discovered that he was in a temporary alliance with the [[Margaster Family|Margasters]], that provided him with various human test subjects in return for him giving the [[Margaster Family|Margasters]]' his most prized creation: the [[Greater Flesh Golem]]
 
This flesh golem he gave the [[Margaster Family]] was used to infiltrate the [[Order of the Golden Lion]] as an assistant to [[High Supervisor Aelfred]].
 
Sometime in 1486 DR, the [[Margaster Family|Margasters]] ordered Mortimer to frame [[Nabulee el Jasafir]] with the murder of [[Archibald Littlefinger|Archibald]]'s son [[Aaron Littlefinger|Aaron]]. He managed to achieve this through the use of spells that modified memory, the use of flesh golems imitating [[Nabulee el Jasafir]] to incriminate him and by planting a deadly item on the auction front that was sold to [[Aaron Littlefinger|Aaron]].
 
Eventually, the [[Seekers]] traced their investigation to [[Mortimer]] and confronted him in his lair with the assistance from an old friend of [[Korosu]] named [[Hiroyashi]]. [[Mortimer]] became extremely powerful thanks to absorbing the corrupting power of blood magic, but he was eventually killed by the group with some help from [[Hiroyashi]] and [[Sir Victorum]], who arrived in the middle of the battle.
