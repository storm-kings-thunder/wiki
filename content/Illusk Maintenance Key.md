A maintenance key bearing a cage symbol, found by the [[Seekers]] in a chest in a room containing statues of priests in [[Illusk]]. Old, deteriorated chalk was also present.

[[Connor Lycanmor|Connor]] used it to open a maintenance cage that serves as a lift to a lower room containing control consoles. The lift has a panel in the language of [[Netheril]] and runes that power its gears when supplied with sufficient energy.
