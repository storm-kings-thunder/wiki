A political alliance of major powers on the Sword Coast. Investigators such as [[Inquisitor Valerius]] operate under its authority.

[[Gundarlun]] is an Alliance island actively fighting pirates at sea. Two operatives were sent to [[Luskan, the city of Sails|Luskan]] on a reconnaissance mission; both returned alive and reported nothing unusual, but [[Tairn Hornblade]] considers those memories false — memory manipulation is active in Luskan.

The [[Seekers]] hold a [[Lords' Alliance Sending Stone]], an Alliance emblem that grants them limited standing and one Sending per day to Valerius.
