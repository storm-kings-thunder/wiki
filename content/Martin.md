Martin was the amnesiac identity imposed upon [[Alkhazir]] by [[Athrax Volderak]]. In this form, he appeared as a grey humanoid of no recognizable race and served as the eternal butler of the [[Extradimensional Mansion]], apparently for thousands of years. He remembered nothing of his life before entering its service.

He served each owner eagerly and loyally, even when subjected to abuse by masters such as [[Elgion Valesworn]]. When treated kindly, he received even small gestures of affection as precious gifts.

During the journey to the [[Desert]], Martin was the first to discover [[Zenvalros]]' emerging [[Vampirism]] and promised to try to keep it secret from the others. He also identified Athrax Volderak as the mansion's first owner and presumed creator.

The [[Seekers]] eventually discovered his true identity. Alkhazir has since apparently regained his original consciousness and rejects the name Martin and the servile existence associated with it.
