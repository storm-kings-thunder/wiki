A mysterious gladiator in the [[Arena]]. Though she appears as an elf, several unsettling features mark her as something more: deformed ear tips, an eye with two pupils, and a smile that disturbs even hardened warriors. Originally a member of the backup team, she was promoted after [[Enshin, the Bloody|Enshin]]'s death.

She worshiped an entity known as the [[God of the Desert]], whom she describes through the dual aspects of "The Waters of Life" and "The Solar Inferno." Most notably, she possesses the ability to perceive the divine or supernatural patrons of the [[Seekers]].

Died after being possessed by the [[God of the Desert|Unconquerable Sun]] in the expedition of the [[Seekers]], the [[Lords of Calimsharn]] and some gladiators to the [[Blood Mines]].
