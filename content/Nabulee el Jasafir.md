
Nabulee is a middle-aged Calishite merchant who established a well-known bazaar in [[Waterdeep]] using the profits from a tragic and controversial event. Some time earlier, in his small shop in [[Calimsharn]], an adventurer collapsed and died unexpectedly. Nabulee sold the man's cache of magical items to start his trading business.

Over the years, Nabulee’s bazaar flourished, becoming one of the most frequented markets for rare magical goods in [[Waterdeep]]. Much of his inventory came from an aging adventurer named [[Mortimer]], who supplied him with relics scavenged from forgotten ruins.

In 1486 DR, Nabulee was framed for the murder of [[Archibald]]’s son. The plot was orchestrated by [[Mortimer]] himself, who had been bribed by the [[Margaster Family|Margasters]] to betray his associate. With the intervention of the [[Seekers]], the truth was uncovered, clearing Nabulee’s name. Grateful and enraged by the [[Margaster Family|Margasters]]’ manipulation, Nabulee vowed to stand against them alongside the [[Seekers]], [[Sir Victorum]], and [[Archibald]].

He later aided the party during the battle against the [[Four Kings]] and the [[Margaster Family|Margasters]].
