Illusk is an ancient [[Netheril|Netherese]] settlement beneath [[Luskan, the city of Sails|Luskan]]. [[Razzles]] believes a hidden faction operates below the city from this buried settlement. An [[Unnamed Luskan Contact|unidentified contact]] asked the [[Seekers]] to travel here and retrieve an old friend or the golden earring from that friend's left ear.

Illusk was an ancient city with Netherese ties, on whose ruins [[Luskan, the city of Sails|Luskan]] was later built. Ruins and underground passages survive beneath the later city. The [[Seekers]] entered after their second assault on [[Kurth's Tower]]. [[Kurth's Earring]] appears to provide entry. Beyond a tunnel, they found a statue of a male humanoid and encountered a ghost rising from the ground who treated them as ambassadors of Illusk.

The city appears carved out and divided into rooms, with a scrambled layout: rooms connect in ways that make little sense, water flows upward, and later repairs patch some of the disruptions. Memory-stealing leeches attacked the party in a flooded apartment room.

A room resembling a market contained a fallen black lantern that appeared to have been hung within recent decades. Another held statues of priests and a chest containing an [[Illusk Maintenance Key|Illusk maintenance key]], alongside old, deteriorated chalk. Around a dry fountain stood four statues: the northern one blind, the western one laughing, the eastern one screaming, and the southern one veiled. Their recesses appeared larger than the statues themselves.

[[Darvan]], a ghost, is in an eastern room of the city.

An unnamed drow woman, accompanied by soldiers armed with crossbows, told the [[Seekers]] that the ruins are near the [[Underdark]]. Arriving with [[Kurth]] or [[Kurth's Earring|his earring]] allows passage to the lower levels, where her group's main base occupies the deepest level. Their leader wears a feathered hat; his name is not yet known to the party. She warned of creatures and living spells caused by the chaos in the ruins, and said the ancient city's defences should leave travellers alone if they follow the obvious route.

[[Connor Lycanmor|Connor]] used the [[Illusk Maintenance Key]] to open a maintenance cage that serves as a lift. Its panel is in the language of [[Netheril]], and inactive runes can power its gears when supplied with enough energy. Old secondary markings lie near the runes. The central square or a dedicated power area may supply the energy; the source is unconfirmed.

The party made the lift descend to a room with control consoles. A word glows on the southern console, and blue energy flows into other rooms; the consoles appear to control the area, whose ecosystem is malfunctioning. Investigating energy in a pit exposed [[Red, the Kid|Red]] to [[Arcane Radiation|arcane radiation]]. Nearby, the party found what appears to be [[Illusk's Vault|the vault]], which Red opened, releasing [[Hardan Voss]].
