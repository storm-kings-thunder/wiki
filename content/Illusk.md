Illusk is an ancient [[Netheril|Netherese]] city buried beneath [[Luskan, the city of Sails|Luskan]], near the [[Underdark]]. Its machinery draws power from the [[Intelligence from the Stars]], a stellar creature imprisoned in [[Illusk's Vault]]. A drow faction occupies the deepest level of the ruins.

## The ruins

Illusk's layout is distorted. Rooms connect in unexpected ways, water flows upward, and later repairs compensate for some of the damage. Dangerous creatures and living spells roam the city, including leeches that steal memories.

The ruins contain former dwellings, a market, and rooms with statues of priests. A dry fountain is surrounded by four statues facing the cardinal directions: a blind figure to the north, a laughing one to the west, a screaming one to the east, and a veiled one to the south. Each stands in a recess larger than the statue itself.

[[Darvan]], a ghost who maps the city's changing layout, occupies an eastern room. He remembers Illusk's collapse and the tyranny of [[Hardan Voss]] after the [[Cataclysm]].

## Access and inhabitants

[[Kurth's Earring]] grants passage through the ruins. A ghost at the entrance treated its bearers as ambassadors of Illusk, and a drow woman confirmed that visitors accompanied by [[Kurth]] or carrying his earring may enter the lower levels.

The woman belonged to a group led by a figure wearing a feathered hat, whose name remains unknown to the party. Their main base lies on the deepest level, near the Underdark. She warned the [[Seekers]] about the dangers roaming the ruins but said the city's ancient defences should leave them alone if they followed the obvious route.

## The Seekers' expedition

[[Razzles]] suspected that a hidden faction operated beneath Luskan. An [[Unnamed Luskan Contact|unidentified contact]] also directed the Seekers here, asking them to retrieve an old friend or the golden earring from his left ear. After their second assault on [[Kurth's Tower]], the party obtained the earring and entered Illusk.

While exploring, they recovered an [[Illusk Maintenance Key]]. [[Connor Lycanmor|Connor]] used it to open a maintenance lift with Netherese controls and rune-powered gears. The lift carried them down to a control room, where blue energy flowed into other parts of the city.

Investigating an energy pit exposed [[Red, the Kid|Red]] to [[Arcane Radiation|arcane radiation]]. Apparently under mental influence, he became intent on opening the vault and using its contents to help his clan. He operated the southern console and released Hardan Voss, who had fused with the stellar creature during his imprisonment. The creature had been feeding on Hardan's consciousness, and the fused being emerged capable of opening fractures in reality.
