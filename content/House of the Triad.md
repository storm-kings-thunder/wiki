[[Bryn Shander]]'s largest place of worship, the only one that truly deserves to be called a temple.

The House of the Triad stands about halfway between the southwest gate and the central market. It honors the three gods known as the Triad

**[[Tyr]]**, the god of justice
**[[Torm]]**, the god of courage and self-sacrifice
and **[[Ilmater]]**, the god of endurance in the face of suffering

## Architecture

Built out of stone, Dwarven style. The stone building that became the House of the Triad was originally built by the dwarves of Kelvin's Cairn.

## Denizens

The temple is attended and maintained by visiting priests and acolytes from Neverwinter and Waterdeep, who usually stay for no more than two years before returning whence they came.
