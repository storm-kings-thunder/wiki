The so-called Tournament is a grand quadrennial event held in **[[Waterdeep]]**, featuring multiple contests where warriors, bards, and mages from across the Sword Coast gather to showcase their talents.

In 1486 DR, the halfling **[[Simon Sack-of-Potatoes]]** sought the help of the **[[Seekers]]** after discovering a cryptic note stating that someone named [[Felix]] had to die during the tournament.

Through investigation, the party uncovered a conspiracy orchestrated by the [[Margaster Family|Margasters]] to manipulate public perception — a plot designed to make [[Sir Egan]] appear as a hero of the people and an ideal puppet for the family’s political ambitions.

[[Felix]], it turned out, was not a person but a powerful arcane construct built by the mercenary dwarven clan [[Hexhammer Clan]]. It had been programmed to attack the tournament so that Sir Egan, wielding _rust monster antenna swords_, could theatrically “defeat” it before the crowd.

## Tournament Events (1486 DR)

### 🏹 The Archery Contest

A classic archery competition consisting of an elimination round followed by one-on-one duels to test accuracy and composure.  
**Winner:** [[Zenvalros]]

---

### 🎵 The Clash of Poets

A musical and performance-based contest featuring:

- A singing elimination round.
- A talent showcase finale.

**Winner:** [[Solomon Pyllartes|Solomon]]

**Notable Incident:**  
One participant, [[Connor Lycanmor]], was possessed by [[Ulgyr]] and proceeded to defile the stage. He was promptly jailed for a day.

---

### 🐎 The Horse Race

A sprawling obstacle course with dozens of contestants.

**Winner:** [[Red, the Kid|Red]], who ingeniously used [[Connor Lycanmor|Connor]] (in his bear form) as his mount.

**Notable Incident:**  
[[Red, the Kid|Red]] was nearly deceived by a tiefling named [[Ammon-Hesh]] into feeding his “mount” a suspicious speed potion.

---

### ✨ The Spellcasting Contest

- Round one: display of raw destructive magical power.
- Round two: one-on-one duels to determine the champion.

**Winner:** [[Diz Iberon]] apprentice of [[Elminster]], who participated solely for the prize money.  
He later gifted a mysterious magical cube to Solomon, who placed second after using Suggestion on [[Willow Margaster|Willow]].

---

### 🍳 The Cooking Show

A culinary challenge where two teams were tasked with creating the best dish possible using particularly unpleasant ingredients.

**Host:** [[Pierre Fierren]], the famed inventor of _pizza_.  
**Judges:**
- _[[Goredawn Ramsay]]_ — ruthless but renowned chef.
- _Lerime Essagal_ — elven culinary critic.
- _Gimble Jimblebottom_ — gnomish gourmand.

**Winning Team:** [[Connor Lycanmor|Connor]], [[Ougigoth Togak|Nog]], and [[Morrigan Volderak|Morrigan]], who impressed the judges with a soufflé made from a Nothic’s eye.  
**Losing Team:** [[Solomon Pyllartes|Solomon]], [[Willow]], and [[Simon Sack-of-Potatoes]], whose basilisk-heart dish nearly choked Gimble to death.

---

### 🥧 The Pie Eating Contest

A light-hearted (and gluttonous) event where most participants joined for the free food.

**Winner:** [[Ougigoth Togak|Nog Buzzkiller]]

---

### 🏇 The Joust

The first phase required participants to capture their own mounts from the surrounding fields.

**Highlights:**

- Both [[Ougigoth Togak|Nog]] and [[Connor Lycanmor|Connor]] coincidentally captured camels
- [[Connor Lycanmor|Connor]]’s camel proved to be a bloodthirsty beast, while [[Ougigoth Togak|Nog]]’s camel claimed [[Connor Lycanmor|Connor]]’s had killed its parents.
- The ensuing camel vendetta ended with [[Connor Lycanmor|Connor]]’s victory, though his mount nearly killed [[Ougigoth Togak|Nog]]’s

---

### ⚔️ The Grand Tournament

A prestigious one-on-one dueling competition concluding the event.

**Final Duel:** [[Willow Margaster|Willow]] vs [[Ougigoth Togak|Nog]]  
**Winner:** [[Willow Margaster|Willow]], by a narrow of margins.

---

### 💥 The Battle Royale

An all-out melee featuring nearly a hundred participants.

The match was abruptly interrupted by the arrival of [[Felix]], the arcane construct built by the [[Hexhammer Clan]].  
It was “defeated” by [[Sir Egan]] in a staged battle — a moment that ultimately led to the revelation of the [[Margaster Family|Margasters]]’ scheme and their downfall.
