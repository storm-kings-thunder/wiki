Tiamat is the goddess of evil dragons and queen of chromatic dragonkind. She takes the form of a vast dragon with five heads, one for each chromatic colour: black, blue, green, red, and white. She believes dragons should rule the mortal world and demands worship befitting her power.

In 1483 DR, the [[Black Talon]] claimed to have uncovered a [[Cult of Tiamat]] among the kobold tribes beneath [[Waterdeep]]. The accusation brought an armed response, though the kobolds' actual allegiance remains uncertain.
