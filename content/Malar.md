Used to be the primal God of Beasts before his demise 5,000 years ago. Malar created six demigod-like entities of beasthood, with a seventh, [[Ulvar]] forming from the leftover energy. These entities formed a pact with an ancient noble family through an unknown intermediary. As a result from this pact, the [[Seven Sons of Malar]] each possessed a single individual who were used to fighting on the frontlines against a mysterious army of undead.

Not much is known about the outcome of this battle, but it is known that [[Ulvar]], the weakest one, was defeated in battle and presumed dead.
