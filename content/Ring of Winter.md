The Ring of Winter is a legendary artifact of powerful cold magic, kept by [[Artus Cimber]]. It grants its wearer immunity to cold, conceals them from divination magic, and prevents them from aging.

Artus discovered the ring after years of searching in the jungles of Chult. In 1485 DR, Frost Giants seeking the artifact attacked [[Bryn Shander]] in an unsuccessful attempt to find him.
