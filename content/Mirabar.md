Mirabar is a wealthy mining city on the north bank of the River Mirar, on the northern Sword Coast. By the late 14th century DR it was the richest city north of [[Waterdeep]], exporting metals, gemstones, and cut stone sourced from mines deep in the Spine of the World.

The city is home to a substantial population of shield dwarves who live and work underground alongside humans. True power lies with the Council of Sparkling Stones, a mixed dwarven-human elected assembly that sets production quotas, though the city is nominally ruled by a hereditary marchion.

Stone quarried in Mirabar is traded to [[Waterdeep]] via [[Luskan, the city of Sails|Luskan]], and Mirabar-crafted goods are imported as far as [[Bryn Shander]].
