Following a posthumous request from the [[Lord of Knowledge]], the [[Seekers]] traveled to the [[Luminous Cloud Citadel]] to intervene in the crisis within the [[Order of the Purple Robes]]. Its founder, [[Eszteban, the Magnificent|Eszteban]], had sealed himself inside the upper levels with the [[Discoveries]].

## Major Events

- [[Magister Lavinia Brightswan|Lavinia]] asked the party to recover the Discoveries and persuade Eszteban to rejoin the Order.
- Negotiations exposed rivalries among the mages. [[Baelgrak]] defended Eszteban's earlier ideals, while [[Hrimmar]] sought control of the discoveries for himself.
- The party explored the sealed tower and found the body of [[Valkas Skyrgela]].
- In the upper library, [[Meredin]] revealed himself and killed Eszteban.
- Meredin's role in poisoning Eszteban under a deal with [[Father Rogers]] became known, and he was arrested.

## Consequences

The Order replaced its single-leader structure with a governing council. The Seekers gained continuing library access, subject to the Order's terms for sharing new spells.

[[Maerik 'Andarilho' Brightpeak|Maerik]] began learning [[Truespeech]] through teachings hidden in the Discoveries. He later represented the Order in partnership negotiations with the [[Arcane Academy]] in [[Silverymoon]].

## Unresolved Threads

Meredin's ultimate fate remained subject to the council's decision. Father Rogers's influence and the consequences of Eszteban's research remained concerns.
