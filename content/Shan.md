A woman who meditates at a four-elements training monastery. She observes that [[Zenvalros]] has a "fire" inside him unlike ordinary practitioners — he does not imitate the elements, he _is_ them. She warns he may cross a line most people never approach, and that the [[Volcanic Bracers]] are opening a door that most do not know exists.

She knew [[Master Amon]], the former master of Zenvalros's order in [[Neverwinter]], and describes him as a man who left with absolute resolve, apparently willing to pay whatever price was required to transform himself into a tool.
