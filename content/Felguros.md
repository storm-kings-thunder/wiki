Felguros is a brass dragon and ally of the [[Seekers]], who cast a True Polymorph-like spell on himself to become a halfling bard. He has a slight tendency to steal valuables.

In 1485 DR, he assisted the founding members of the [[Seekers]] in repelling a small orc invasion of [[Nightstone]], forming a friendship that has continued since.

In 1486 DR, he attempted to transform into a halfling so he could accompany the [[Seekers]] as an adventurer — but forgot to learn how to turn back into a dragon. He traveled with the party for a time before parting ways in [[Neverwinter]], promising to meet them near the [[Desert]] to claim the relic held there. Growing impatient, he set out alone, and his unconscious, starving form was eventually found by slavers in the Desert and brought to [[Calimsharn]] to fight as a gladiator.

He has since managed to regain his ability to transform back into a dragon.

His former lair is in [[Askor]], where his horde is currently guarded by two blue dragons. One of the giant relics sought by the party is there.
