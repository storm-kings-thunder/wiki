Four small stones given to the [[Seekers]] by [[Tairn Hornblade]], High Mage of [[Silverymoon]]. Each stone continuously stores the last five minutes of the bearer's memory and can be accessed at any time.

Intended as a safeguard against the memory manipulation active in [[Luskan, the city of Sails|Luskan]], where [[Lords Alliance]] operatives returned with false recollections after a mission they believed went normally.

After reuniting with the party at [[Kurth's Tower]], [[Connor Lycanmor|Connor]] used a stone and saw himself speaking with [[Kurth]], who asked him to attack the Seekers in the tower.
