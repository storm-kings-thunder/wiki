High Mage of [[Silverymoon]]. He summoned the [[Seekers]] via tattooed warriors using a claimed inconsistency in their stated origin as a pretext — the real purpose was to pass on intelligence privately.

He warned the party that the [[Lords Alliance]] sent two operatives to [[Luskan, the city of Sails|Luskan]]; both returned alive and reported nothing unusual. Tairn considers those reports false — the operatives' memories of the city cannot be trusted. He gave the party four [[Anchor Stones]] as a precaution against the memory manipulation active in Luskan.
