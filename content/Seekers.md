Most members of the adventuring party are currently separated, scattered across the continent.

Before the operation at [[Askor]], the party acquired a carriage and supplies, then later established temporary lodging in an inn in the middle district of [[Calimsharn]].

The active group has returned to [[Luskan, the city of Sails|Luskan]]. After securing a naval and commercial agreement with [[Taerl]], they entered [[Kurth's Tower]] and reunited with [[Connor Lycanmor|Connor]], breaking the curse compelling him to attack them. They withdrew without defeating [[Varlak]], returned to [[Don Tigre's Hideout]], and rested; [[Red, the Kid|Red]] informed [[Razzles]] of Connor's return.

The following day, they met [[Kessara Vold]] at the [[Administrative Tower]]. She offered a satisfactory position for Don Tigre's clan in exchange for identifying certain people and explicitly sought [[Krieg]]'s removal. No acceptance of her proposal is recorded.

Their working Luskan plan is to reunite [[Red, the Kid|Red]]'s divided family, then decide whether to unite the [[Pirate Lords]] against the [[Red Wizards of Thay]] or feign cooperation with the [[Red Wizards of Thay|Red Wizards]] before betraying them in coordination with the [[Pirate Lords]].

The group returned to [[Kurth's Tower]] and obtained [[Kurth's Earring]] by cutting off his ear, revealing him as a drow apparently formerly called Beniago. They then entered [[Illusk]], explored its distorted rooms, encountered memory-stealing leeches, and found an [[Illusk Maintenance Key|Illusk maintenance key]]. There they met [[Darvan]].

In Illusk, a drow woman told them that her group's main base is on the deepest level near the [[Underdark]]. She described its leader as wearing a feathered hat; his name remains unknown. [[Connor Lycanmor|Connor]] used the maintenance key to open a lift, which the party took down to a control room. After exposure to [[Arcane Radiation|arcane radiation]], [[Red, the Kid|Red]] appeared mentally manipulated into opening [[Illusk's Vault]] to help his clan. He operated the southern console and released [[Hardan Voss]], now capable of opening fractures in reality.
