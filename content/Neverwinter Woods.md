Located in the North, Neverwinter Woods is a vast and ancient forest suffused with a quiet, ever-present sense of magic. Beneath that magic, there is a lingering corruption that twists the natural order.
 
## Temple to [[Silvanus, God of Nature]]

In the woods is located a hidden temple to [[Silvanus, God of Nature|Silvanus]], which can be accessed by his followers by entering the roots of trees in the forest. The leader of this temple was the archdruid [[Archdruid Oloamin|Oloamin]], one of [[Silvanus, God of Nature|Silvanus]]' greatest followers.

In 1480 DR, the corruption in the core of the forest started to expand, causing the slowly growing cancer spreading throughout the entire forest, stalled only by the influence of [[Archdruid Oloamin|Oloamin]]. In 1486 DR, [[Archdruid Oloamin|Oloamin]] sent [[Ellora Oak]], member of the [[Seekers]] and follower of [[Silvanus, God of Nature|Silvanus]], to cleanse this corruption along with the rest of the party. After managing to destroy the source of the corruption, a powerful necromantic gem, the cursed creatures and trees of the forest were quickly purified, or disintegrated if the corruption ran too deeply. As thanks, [[Silvanus, God of Nature|Silvanus]] used his power to turn [[Ellora Oak|Ellora]]'s staff into the [[Heart of the Woodlands]], a powerful legacy weapon.

## The Mansion of [[Monthartas]]

The place which spawned the corruption. It was originally a noble house belonging to the [[Monthartas]] family.

In 1455 DR, the hag [[Gertrude]] infiltrated the mansion disguised as an advisor. Throughout the years, she managed to corrupt the General Monthartas and led him to commit terrible deeds. When he sacrificed his daughter, the curse became completed and turned the mansion into an aberration of necromantic energies.

In 1486 DR, the [[Seekers]] managed to kill [[Gertrude]] inside the mansion, and then destroy the crystal she created to spread the corruption
