A magical earring taken by the [[Seekers]] when they cut off [[Kurth]]'s ear during their second assault on [[Kurth's Tower|his tower]]. Removing it revealed that its wearer is a drow, apparently formerly called Beniago. *Identify* revealed that the earring always gives its wearer Kurth's appearance.

It also appears to grant entry to [[Illusk]]. A ghost encountered there treated the party as though they were ambassadors of the city.

An unnamed drow woman in Illusk confirmed that arriving with either [[Kurth]] or the earring permits passage to the lower levels, where her group has its main base.
