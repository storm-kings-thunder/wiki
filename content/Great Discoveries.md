An arcane initiative discussed by [[Eszteban, the Magnificent]] and [[Lord of Knowledge|Professor Ishmael]], focused on compiling a major archive of spells and magical findings since the founding of the [[Order of the Purple Robes]].

Within the order, the term also refers to newly created spells and original arcane breakthroughs.

The discoveries are tied to a magical book that appears to visually distort reality and cause headaches when observed directly. Control of the discoveries became the core dispute among members of the [[Order of the Purple Robes]] during the crisis.

A physical book found in [[Eszteban, the Magnificent|Eszteban]]'s research room was an acid trap with no useful content — the real discoveries are housed in the upper library of the [[Luminous Cloud Citadel]], accessible to the party as part of the agreement with the [[Order of the Purple Robes]].
