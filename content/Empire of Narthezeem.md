The Empire of Narthezeem is an obscure empire of [[Animal-headed Demigods]] who fell 10.000 years ago due to what is described in translated texts as [[The Ebon Flame]]. Only a few ruins are proof of its existence, but the powerful magical relics found in them prove that this empire was a powerful one. It is known that [[Alkhazir]] was connected to the Empire in the decades before their fall, so some rumors theorize that Alkhazir was somehow responsible for the Empire's fall.

The [[Lord of Knowledge]] explains that the demigods of Narthezeem were organized into castes: the crocodile figure belongs to the caste that governs the River.
