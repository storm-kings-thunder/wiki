The Blacklake District is the northern district of [[Neverwinter]], named after the eponymous Blacklake. Before the destruction of the city in 1451 DR, it was inhabited by nobles who built with quality materials and protected their homes with magic, leaving it largely intact after the disasters that struck the city. Afterward, most buildings were repurposed as makeshift barracks or fell into disrepair.

## Locations

**Hallowed Temple of Selûne**
- Temple of Selûne with a small but capable membership, able to remove most curses.
- In 1486 DR, a ritual was attempted here to remove the Kraken's curse afflicting Leor, a member of [[Seekers]].

**Beached Leviathan**
- A tavern built inside a shipwrecked vessel that ran aground before the Docks were rebuilt. [[Harrag]], its former pirate captain, refurbished it into a popular tavern.
- The floors are named nautically: "the Hold" (kitchen and bar), "the Quarters" (inn with nine rooms, two suites, and a mid-sized room), and "the Deck" (seating and private parlors).
- A hub for trade deals, stolen goods, information exchange, and plotting of all kinds.
- In 1486 DR, it hosted a gambling tournament attended by some of the [[Seekers]] and was later attacked by Grothgar.

**Gnobbar and Gnubbar's Mechanical Marvels**
- A ramshackle shop run by eccentric gnome twins [[Gnobbar]] and Gnubbar, used to test and sell their inventions.
- The [[Seekers]] purchased a chain mace, a repeater crossbow, and a spy fly here in 1486 DR. [[Ougigoth Togak]] borrowed the shop's best weapon for the arena fights.
- In 1486 DR, Gnubbar was killed during Grothgar's rampage and buried in the Neverdeath Graveyard.

**The Arm and Hammer**
- A blacksmith owned by Bob Stevens, a war veteran who lost an arm serving with the army of [[Mithral Hall]]. He uses runic talents to craft powerful magical weapons.
- In 1486 DR, he lost his other arm during Grothgar's rampage.
