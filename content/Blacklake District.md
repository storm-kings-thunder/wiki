The Blacklake District occupies northern [[Neverwinter]] and takes its name from the lake within it. Before [[Mount Hotenow]] erupted in 1451 DR, it was home to wealthy nobles whose well-built, magically protected estates survived much of the destruction. Many were later converted into makeshift barracks or left to decay.

## Locations

- **[[Hallowed Temple of Selûne]].** A small temple whose clergy can lift most curses.
- **[[Beached Leviathan]].** A shipwreck converted into an inn and tavern by [[Harag|Harrag]], frequented by merchants, smugglers, and informants.
- **[[Gnobbar and Gnubbar's Mechanical Marvels]].** A workshop selling experimental inventions, founded by the gnome twins [[Gnobbar]] and [[Gnubbar]].
- **[[The Arm and Hammer]].** A smithy owned by [[Bob Stevens]], a veteran who crafts enchanted weapons using runic magic.
