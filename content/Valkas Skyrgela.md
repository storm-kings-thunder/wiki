A wizard and member of the [[Order of the Purple Robes]].

Found dead in the left (cold) corridor of the [[Luminous Cloud Citadel]], killed by extreme cold approximately one week before the [[Seekers]] arrived. He was still clutching a magical +1 defensive Quarterstaff in the style worn by the order's wizards, and had a dagger with a broken tip in his right hand. Before dying, he wrote *"Metal plaque"* on the wall.

His portrait hangs in the Citadel's research room gallery alongside those of [[Magister Lavinia Brightswan|Lavinia]] and [[Eszteban, the Magnificent|Eszteban]].
