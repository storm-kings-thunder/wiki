Gorm is a huge, shadowy knight armed with a Greataxe, hunting Morrigan of [[Seekers]]. He is drawn to [[Morrigan Volderak]].

His origins are mostly unknown, except the image of an executioner conjured by Solomon's cube. He apparently seeks the death of anyone bearing the surname of Volderak and is relentless in his pursuit.

When Morrigan left [[Waterdeep]] with the party, the protection she had from this entity disappeared and she started receiving visions of his coming. They first saw him on the ruins of [[Nightstone]] after he crushed the corpse of its mayor. There, [[Seekers]] fought and defeated a relentless beast who ignored all wounds to attack Morrigan.

After his "death," the party continued their journey east and discovered that their memories of [[Nightstone]] were almost erased, revealing Gorm's ability to disintegrate things or people from existence itself.

He returned alive once again to fight [[Seekers]] shortly after their escape from the Enclave of Kelemvor. He was quickly defeated again, but this showed that Gorm is apparently an extremely powerful and ancient revenant who will not stop returning stronger than before until Morrigan dies.

During one of [[Seekers]]' journeys through the Desert, they saw Gorm following them in the distance. Thankfully, the Crawling Disease saw Gorm as a vulnerable prey and attacked him, allowing the group to continue on their journey.
