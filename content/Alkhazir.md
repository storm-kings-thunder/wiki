Alkhazir is said to be the founder of [[Alchemy]] and creator of the [[Emerald Tablet]], an artifact even greater than the famed [[Philosopher's Stone]]. It is known that Alkhazir lived around 10,000 years ago during the time of [[Empire of Narthezeem]] and was connected to them at a later point of the empire. Before the Fall of Narthezeem, he vanished, leaving rumors of the [[Emerald Tablet]] and speculation that he was responsible for the catastrophe.

The [[Seekers]] discovered that Alkhazir's disappearance followed his defeat by [[Athrax Volderak]]. Athrax stripped him of his memories and sealed him in the form of Martin, an unrecognizable grey humanoid cursed to serve the owners of the [[Extradimensional Mansion]] eternally.

Alkhazir has apparently regained his original consciousness and now stands guard over the nearly complete [[Emerald Tablet]] within the ruins of the [[Temple of Narthezeem]].  He resents having been forced into the identity of "[[Alkhazir|Martin]]." He dislikes being referred to as Martin and has made clear he does not want to return to that servile existence. He explains that the [[Seekers]] have likely been scattered across the world now after the events in the [[Temple of Narthezeem]].
