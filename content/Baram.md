Baram is one of the surviving [[Pirate Lords]] of [[Luskan, the city of Sails|Luskan]] and represents their main military force in the city. His territory includes the docks, but he has withdrawn into his fortress while the [[Red Wizards of Thay]] tighten their control.

[[Razzles]] believes Baram has a vendetta against [[Krieg]] and may be manipulable.
