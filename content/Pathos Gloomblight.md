A necromancer harboring a deep resentment toward [[Elminster]], whom he accused of stealing his research during their university years. When the [[Margaster Family|Margasters]] offered him a contract to assassinate [[Elminster]], he accepted without hesitation. After imprisoning [[Elminster]]’s soul in a gem, he was defeated by the [[Seekers]], and his corpse was devoured by [[Connor Lycanmor]].
