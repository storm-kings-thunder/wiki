Krieg is a one-armed, one-eyed veteran from [[Luskan]] and one of the first members of the [[Don Tigre]] clan. In 1445 DR, a few years after joining the clan, Krieg became the second-in-command of the bandit clan. Being a constant presence in the life of Redd the Kidd, Krieg quickly became a surrogate grandfather of sorts, like [[Don Tigre]] is considered the father figure.

As tensions grew between the [[Don Tigre]] clan and the [[Pirate Lords]], Krieg's desire of revenge against them slowly grew and the relationship between him and [[Don Tigre]] slowly deteriorated because of his cautious and non-violent methodology against the [[Pirate Lords]].

In 1486 DR, he managed to lead a small group to prevent the [[Seekers]]' deaths by an ambush/trap from the [[Pirate Lords]]' assassins. He later used the opportunity to lead a large part of [[Don Tigre]]'s forces to ambush their outposts while they were unaware, and managed to deal a heavy blow against them.

As of now, a schism has formed within the clan, with Krieg leading greater numbers in more violent attacks against the [[Pirate Lords]] while [[Don Tigre]] attempts to maintain stability. Only the respect that the two leaders hold for each other prevents an actual inner war.

Human Illuskan male. Alignment: Chaotic Neutral.
