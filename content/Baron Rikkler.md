Baron Rikkler is a noble from [[Bryn Shander]] who visited [[Neverwinter]] in 1486 DR. During the [[Harvest Festival]] in the [[Docks District]], the [[Killer Clowns of Waterdeep]] tried to assassinate him as part of a plot within [[Don Tigre]]'s clan to provoke a war between the two cities. The [[Seekers]] foiled the attempt after [[Razzles]] warned them of the plot.
