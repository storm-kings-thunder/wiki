Ogden Flamebeard is the tavern owner of [[Kelvin's Comfort]] in [[Bryn Shander]], the most known drinking place of the city, famous for his Flamebeard's Firebrandy drink.

In his youth, Ogden had worked in many mines and had grown a network of contacts in all dwarfholds of the North, including [[Mithral Hall]].

Shield Dwarf male. Alignment: Neutral Good.
