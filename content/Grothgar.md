Grothgar was a bloodthirsty orc warrior from the old [[Togak Tribe]] where [[Ougigoth Togak|Nog Buzzkiller]] was born in. Though renowned as one of their greatest fighters, Grothgar’s unrestrained savagery proved too much even for his own kin. His insatiable lust for violence eventually led to his exile — a rare punishment among orc tribes.
 
Some time later, he was offered power by a powerful divine entity of war in exchange for spreading its influence across the mortal world. For this purpose, he joined a "group of assassins" from [[Don Tigre]]'s clan. 

Their mission: assassinate [[Baron Rikkler]], a visiting noble in [[Neverwinter]].
When the plan failed, Grothgar took matters into his own hands. He decided to start a war on his own terms and started massacring hundreds of citizens of Neverwinter, until [[Dagult Neverember]] wouldn't have a choice except declare war against [[Luskan, the city of Sails|Luskan]].
 
Thankfully, his massacre was prevented by the visiting [[Seekers]], but not before maiming and killing some allies of the group, such as the gnome [[Gnubbar]] and the dwarf blacksmith [[Bob Stevens]]. 

Upon his death, the power lent to him by the entity attempted to possess [[Zenvalros]]. Only by making a pact with an entity calling itself [[Death]], has he managed to prevent the possession.
