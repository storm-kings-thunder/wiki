Ulvar is a werebat, the weakest of the [[Seven Sons of Malar]]. There were supposed to only exist six Sons, but the leftover energy utilized for their creation led to his creation. 

During the battle against a mysterious army of undead 5,000 years ago, he was defeated in battle and left for dead by [[Ulveric]], who could not afford to waste energy in a crucial moment of the war.
 
He eventually became corrupted by a mysterious entity and became a hybrid between [[Vampirism]] and [[Lycantrophy]], subsuming the consciousness of his original human host. He eventually was trapped and sealed by a group of [[Kelemvor, God of the Dead|Kelemvor]]'s followers in the [[Enclave of Kelemvor]].
 
During the next 5000 years, he eventually spread his corruption throughout the [[Enclave of Kelemvor]] and twisted the servants of [[Kelemvor, God of the Dead|Kelemvor]] into bloodthirsty beasts. When the [[Seekers]] arrived in 1486 DR, they accidentally managed to free him by killing the corrupted keeper of the [[Enclave of Kelemvor|Enclave]].
 
[[Connor Lycanmor]] entered the chamber where [[Ulvar]] was kept and he emerged as an elderly man, now adopting the name of [[Ulvar|Malik]]. When combat inevitably started, he drained the vitality of [[Zenvalros]] to regain his youth, fought and won against [[Connor Lycanmor|Connor]]'s primal bear transformation, due to the rapid regeneration of his wounds. He decided to spare his life because of [[Ulgyr]]'s request, and left the [[Enclave of Kelemvor|Enclave]] in his winged form, but not before recruiting [[Yinsaren]], the only remaining survivor of the Enclave, to his cause.
 
Later in the [[Desert]], after [[Ulgyr]]'s "death", he showed his presence to [[Connor Lycanmor|Connor]] to show respect to his brother's almost death, promising to kill him if [[Ulgyr]] somehow managed to die for real.
