A figure foretold in [[Seraphina]]'s visions — an armoured abyssal warrior who appears at the head of the deep tribes' army, decapitating the queen of [[Seraphina]]'s Sea Elf kingdom.

He is believed to be the exiled demigod of the [[Salt Throne Council]] who once advocated for diplomacy with surface races. Cast out for these views, he gained abyssal power and united the Cnidarans, Coralborn, and Sharkin tribes against the kingdom before vanishing. His return is among the great threats foreseen in the visions.
