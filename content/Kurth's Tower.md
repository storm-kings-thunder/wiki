A four-story building in [[Luskan, the city of Sails|Luskan]] where [[Kurth]] has isolated himself. Its windows are barred and its door is magically sealed.

While searching for a route to the tower, the [[Seekers]] used [[Truespeech|Reveal]] and saw an unnamed surface junk dealer near the sea. When visited, she displayed wands and other objects recovered from the sewers.

The [[Seekers]] forced the door open with *Dispel Magic* after Kurth refused to trust that they were not assassins. Kurth transported himself elsewhere as they entered. Inside, they found an immobile, bloodied human with a glaive in his lap, covered in dust as though long forgotten. His wounds appeared partly self-inflicted and partly external, and he seemed to have died earlier that day, before their arrival; he was actually under *Feign Death*.

They also encountered [[Connor Lycanmor|Connor]], who attacked in bear form under a curse until recognizing [[Red, the Kid|Red]] and [[Zenvalros]] broke its hold. After fighting Kurth's guard [[Varlak]] and the initially hostile Connor, the party withdrew without defeating Varlak, though they had substantially wounded him. Connor later used an [[Anchor Stones|Anchor Stone]] to recall Kurth asking him to attack the party in the tower.

The Seekers returned for a successful second assault, approaching with two members from above and two from below. They found Kurth and cut off the ear bearing [[Kurth's Earring|his earring]], revealing him to be a drow. After regrouping, they travelled to [[Illusk]].
