High Supervisor Aelfred is one of the most powerful living followers of [[Tyr]] and currently leads the [[Order of the Golden Lion]]. In 1470 DR, he adopted [[Morrigan Volderak|Morrigan]] into the Order and taught her to become a powerful paladin. During this time, he learned of a mysterious creature that murdered [[Morrigan Volderak|Morrigan]]'s original parents and begged [[Tyr]] to protect her while she was in [[Waterdeep]]. He also kept [[Morrigan Volderak|Morrigan]]'s family heirloom to give to her when the time was right.

In 1486 DR, [[Sir Egan]] was temporarily admitted to the [[Order of the Golden Lion]] to test his worthiness. Only a few weeks later, it was revealed that Egan was a fraud and the crimes of the [[Margaster Family|Margasters]] were exposed. Upon learning of their crimes, Aelfred stripped the [[Margasters]] of their noble titles and exiled them from [[Waterdeep]]. They did not react well, and an intense battle ensued between the [[Seekers]] and their allies against the two Margasters and the [[Four Kings]]. After the battle, Aelfred also exiled the party from [[Waterdeep]], partly because of minor crimes committed during the conflict and also to move them out of the city until Margaster supporters were rooted out.
 
Before the group left, Aelfred gave [[Morrigan Volderak|Morrigan]] a magical stone to communicate with him and her heirloom: the sword named [[Crownfall]]. During later communications, the group learned that a large wave hit [[Waterdeep]] and caused significant, but repairable, damage to its docks.
