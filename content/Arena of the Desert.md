## Overview

The Arena is the [[Master of the Arena]]'s domain and the main tourist attraction in [[Calimsharn]], located in the city's outermost district. Despite the dangers of crossing the [[Desert]], wealthy patrons travel there with sizable escorts to watch its gladiatorial contests.

The public can access only the Colosseum during combats. The underground levels are reserved for gladiators and staff, with increasingly strict restrictions on the deeper floors.

## Structure

### Colosseum

Far larger than other known arenas on the Sword Coast, the Colosseum holds thousands of spectators. The [[Master of the Arena]] watches from a tall pedestal and can reshape the battlefield through [[Truespeech]]. He was formerly accompanied by the [[Infernal Knight]], who now serves the [[Lord of Slaves]].

Small chambers reserved for gladiators provide space to prepare before combat.

### Floor of Gladiators

A vast underground complex with no visible entrance from outside. Rumor holds that only those marked by the [[Master of the Arena]] can teleport inside.

The level contains a cantina, training grounds, and a tavern, all reserved for gladiators. [[Undan]], a gruff dwarf representing the [[Lords of Calimsharn]], manages the complex and tavern.

### Lower Floor

Cells and cages hold creatures destined for combat, some rumored to be sapient. Access is restricted, though favored gladiators occasionally receive temporary permission to enter.

### Lowest Floor

The deepest level houses monstrosities captured in the [[Desert]] or brought from the [[Pit]], which lies beneath the Arena. Access is highly restricted, though favored gladiators have occasionally visited.

Rumor suggests this floor offers a route into the Pit, potentially explaining the capture of some of its stranger inhabitants. The [[Lord of Science]] also occasionally supplies failed experiments for arena combat.

## The Seekers

After slavers captured [[Felguros]] in the [[Desert]] and brought him to the Arena, the [[Seekers]] negotiated with the [[Lord of Slaves]] for his freedom. Rather than securing his immediate release, they entered a less restrictive contract requiring occasional participation in arena combat until Felguros could be freed.
