**Alignment**: Lawful Neutral
**Category**: None
**Domains**: Arcana, Knowledge
**Pantheon**: Faerûnian
**Province**: Divination, fortunetelling, fate
**Symbol**: Crystal ball containing many kinds of eyes

A god of divination and fortunetelling. Few people worship Savras, but many pray to him when performing small rituals of foresight — for example, young men and women sometimes attempt to divine the names of their future spouses by saying a rhyming chant that calls upon Savras while gazing in a mirror.

Savras has no currently active temples in Faerûn, and his shrines are few and far between, tucked into corners of libraries and scriptoria. Despite this, certain folk pay him regular homage: investigators, diviners, judges, and others who need to uncover the truth. Such individuals can sometimes be identified by the elaborate staffs they carry in his honor.

According to legend, Savras was trapped in [[Azuth]]'s staff for ages. Azuth eventually freed him in exchange for a sworn fealty, and the staff remains a potent symbol for his faithful. Devout worshipers take great pains to decorate and embellish their staffs, each hoping Savras might find it a welcoming place to stop for a time.

Revered by agents such as [[Inquisitor Valerius]].
