A current member of the [[Seekers]] and follower of [[Kelemvor, God of the Dead|Kelemvor]]. She joined [[Maerik 'Andarilho' Brightpeak|Maerik]] as an exceptional healer to help rescue the [[Seekers]] from the [[Temple of Narthezeem]].

She is expected to meet [[Inquisitor Valerius]], a [[Lords Alliance]] investigator linked to [[Savras, God of Divination and Fate|Savras]].

While researching in the [[Luminous Cloud Citadel|Citadel]] library, she found records that the [[Kraken]] can communicate telepathically with cult members and kill them remotely, and that isolated island societies may venerate it entirely.

Her Sea Elf kingdom is governed by the [[Salt Throne Council]], composed of several demigods. Historically, one of those demigods advocated for cooperation with other deep-sea tribes and diplomacy with surface races; he was exiled for it. He then gained abyssal power, united the Cnidarans, Coralborn, and Sharkin tribes under his banner, and launched a war against the kingdom before disappearing. The allied tribes were subsequently expelled. This exiled demigod may be connected to the [[King of the Depths]] seen in her visions.

Looking into the divination orb found in [[Eszteban, the Magnificent|Eszteban]]'s research room, she experienced a series of visions:

- Her family together in simpler times
- The death of her sister
- The [[Kraken]] destroying the world
- An abyssal figure in armour decapitating her mother
- The deep tribes massing an army to conquer her kingdom
- The return of a figure known as the [[King of the Depths]]

She also had a vision at the [[Kelemvor, God of the Dead|Kelemvor]] shrine in [[Silverymoon]], the first miracle.
