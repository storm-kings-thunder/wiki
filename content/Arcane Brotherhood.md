The Arcane Brotherhood is a powerful wizards' guild and mercantile organization based in [[Luskan, the city of Sails|Luskan]]. Its headquarters, the Hosttower of the Arcane, serves as both a center of magical learning and a base for its political and economic influence.

The Brotherhood pursues magical knowledge, wealth, and power. It has a reputation for secrecy and for allowing its members to pursue dangerous or morally questionable research. Historically, it exerted considerable influence over [[Luskan, the city of Sails|Luskan]] and its pirate captains.

[[Kurth]], one of the surviving [[Pirate Lords]], was formerly a member. The Brotherhood's current role under the [[Red Wizards of Thay|Thayan]] domination of Luskan has not been established.
