A vast network of underground tunnels, caverns, and subterranean realms stretching beneath the surface world. Home to numerous dangerous creatures and civilizations, including illithids (mind flayers), drow, and many others.

The illithid [[Karahl-Guhl]] and his companion [[Zaxab]] were exiled from their Underdark enclave before emerging near [[Alkai Tor]] and launching an assault on the village.
