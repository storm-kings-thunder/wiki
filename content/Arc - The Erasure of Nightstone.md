In 1486 DR, [[Gorm]] destroyed the remaining community of [[Nightstone]] to draw the [[Seekers]] back. His attack connected the village's fate to his relentless pursuit of [[Morrigan Volderak|Morrigan]].

## Major Events

- After Morrigan left [[Waterdeep]], the protection that had shielded her from Gorm no longer held.
- While the party was visiting [[Alkai Tor]], Gorm massacred Nightstone's remaining inhabitants.
- The Seekers returned to confront him among the ruins. He ignored his wounds and focused his attacks on Morrigan.
- The party defeated him, but afterward found their memories of Nightstone fading or becoming indistinct.

## Consequences

Nightstone was left uninhabited, and even its keep was disintegrated. The fading memories suggested that Gorm could destroy more than physical lives and buildings.

His defeat did not end the pursuit: Gorm later returned. The full extent of his power and his reasons for hunting the Volderak bloodline remain unresolved.
