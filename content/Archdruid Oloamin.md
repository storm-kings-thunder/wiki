One of [[Silvanus, God of Nature|Silvanus]]' greatest followers, Oloamin led the god's hidden temple in the [[Neverwinter Woods]].

When the corruption at the heart of the forest began spreading in 1480 DR, Oloamin's influence alone held back its advance. In 1486 DR, he sent [[Ellora Oak]] and the rest of the [[Seekers]] to cleanse it.

The Seekers killed [[Gertrude]] in the [[Monthartas]] family mansion and destroyed the necromantic gem responsible for the corruption. The afflicted trees and creatures were purified, or disintegrated where the corruption had taken too deep a hold. In thanks, Silvanus transformed Ellora's staff into the [[Heart of the Woodlands]].
