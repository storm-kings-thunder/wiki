The House of Knowledge is a temple of [[Oghma, God of Knowledge|Oghma]] in [[Neverwinter]]'s [[Protector's Enclave]]. Its arched roof and tall, many-windowed façade make it one of the city's most recognisable buildings. The temple houses Neverwinter's largest library, renowned along the Sword Coast.

The eruption of [[Mount Hotenow]] in 1451 DR badly damaged the temple. It was restored in 1484 DR.
