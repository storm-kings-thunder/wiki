**Alignment**: Lawful Good
**Domains**: War
**Pantheon**: Faerûnian
**Symbol**: White right gauntlet
**Typical Worshipers**: Paladins, soldiers, guards, the dutiful

God of duty, loyalty, courage, and self-sacrifice. Torm was once a mortal hero who achieved divinity. He is the patron deity of paladins and the exemplar of righteous service — those who follow him value obedience to a just cause above personal glory.

Torm is one of the three gods of the Triad, alongside [[Tyr]] and [[Ilmater]], all honored in the [[House of the Triad]] in [[Bryn Shander]].
