The most popular tavern in town, owing to its extensive stock of Dwarven ales and brandies, is Kelvin's Comfort.

The common room is bedecked with Dwarven craft of Battlehammer make, but most of the liquors are imported from [[Mirabar]], on the other side of the Spine of the World.
The one Ten-Towns specialty of note is a treacly mead from Good Mead, a neighboring settlement. Caravan masters and guards with plenty of coin often come here, as do visiting Dwarves from Kelvin's Cairn.

## Denizens

Kelvin's Comfort was owned by shield dwarf [[Ogden Flamebeard]] sometime after the War of the Silver Marches. A frequent patron was [[Baric Nylef]] while he searched for a Dwarven criminal, who was known to be addicted to Flamebeard's Firebrandy.
