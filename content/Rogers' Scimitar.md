[[Father Rogers]] gave this finely crafted scimitar to [[Red, the Kid]] after claiming the soul of the [[Lord of Slaves]]. [[Razzles]] identified it as cursed and tied to Rogers' magical contract, in the same infernal pattern as Rogers' daggers, which send the souls of creatures they kill to Father Rogers.

[[Red, the Kid|Red]] left the scimitar with [[Razzles]] while looking for a way to free himself from Rogers' contract.
