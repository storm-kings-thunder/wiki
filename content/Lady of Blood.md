Real Name: **Kareem el Mustar**

![[Lady of Blood.png|238x357]]

The Lady of Blood oversees both the [[Hospital]] and a portion of the [[Blood Mines]]. In the [[Desert]], where divine magic is absent, she serves a crucial role: treating miners afflicted with the [[Red Plague]] and removing their mutations through her mastery of biological [[Truespeech]], aided by [[Lord of Science|Doctor Craven]]'s vaccine. As the sole accomplished healer in [[Calimsharn]], she vigilantly guards this position of power. In 1486 DR, she enlisted the [[Seekers]] to hunt down a group of escaped patients infected with an unusual strain of [[Vampirism]] who had perpetrated a massacre within the [[Hospital]]. Her protection is ensured by [[Ankhor]], her bodyguard and "son" - a massive humanoid who, despite his vocabulary, maintains a childlike mentality.

She will attend a meeting called by the [[Lord of Knowledge]] provided that [[Seraphina]] leaves the [[Desert]] within one month.
