Artus is an adventurer, former [[Harpers|Harper]] agent, and keeper of the [[Ring of Winter]]. He left the Harpers after becoming disillusioned with their cautious methods.

He spent years searching for the ring before finding it in the jungles of Chult, where he also met and married his wife, [[Alisanda]].

In 1485 DR, Frost Giants attacked [[Bryn Shander]] in search of Artus and the Ring of Winter. The city's inhabitants, aided by adventurers, repelled the attack before he could be found.
