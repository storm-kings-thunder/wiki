A major city in the North, home to the Academy, a prominent center of arcane learning.

The party arrived via teleportation circle directly into the Academy's courtyard. Two knights bearing glowing, magically infused tattoos were present. An apprentice carrying a scroll received the party at arrival. Weapons must be registered at a military checkpoint near the bridge.

Notable locations include the inn known as the Shining Scroll and a [[Kelemvor, God of the Dead|Kelemvor]] shrine tended by an elderly woman. An older [[Astral Ships|astral ship wreck]] lies in the mountains nearby.
