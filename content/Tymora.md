**Alignment**: Chaotic Good
**Domains**: Trickery
**Pantheon**: Faerûnian
**Symbol**: Silver disk (featureless)
**Typical Worshipers**: Adventurers, gamblers, rogues

Goddess of good fortune, skill, and victory. Tymora was born when the original goddess of luck, Tyche, was split in two — Tymora inheriting her good aspect and Beshaba her bad. She is generous with her divine gifts, empowering her followers' luck in moments of great need.

She is the primary deity worshipped in [[Yartar]], where the city's adventurous spirit and lively gambling culture reflect her nature.
