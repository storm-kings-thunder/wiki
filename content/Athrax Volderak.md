Athrax Volderak is supposedly a powerful and ancient king born that ruled a powerful empire around 10.000 years ago before the present day. At some point in his life, it seems that he underwent a transformation into a lich. He also seems to have travelled into the [[Desert]] and defeated [[Alkhazir]] in combat, to then be sealed in the form of [[Alkhazir|Martin]] deprived of its memories and cursed to serve as the butler of the [[Extradimensional Mansion]]. Throughout the years after the death of Athrax, it seems that [[Gorm]] started hunting the remnants of the Volderak bloodline, despite it becoming so diluted. [[Morrigan Volderak]], one of [[Seekers]] is one of those being hunted. He is also said to be the creator and first owner of the [[Extradimensional Mansion]].
