Athrax Volderak is said to have ruled a powerful empire around 10,000 years ago and later become a lich. He is also believed to have created the [[Extradimensional Mansion]] and been its first owner.

He reportedly traveled into the [[Desert]] and defeated [[Alkhazir]] in combat. Athrax then stripped the alchemist of his memories, sealed him in the form of [[Alkhazir|Martin]], and cursed him to serve the mansion's owners eternally.

After Athrax's apparent death, [[Gorm]] began hunting the surviving members of the Volderak bloodline, despite its dilution over generations. [[Morrigan Volderak]], a member of the [[Seekers]], is among those pursued. Gorm's motives and his connection to Athrax remain unclear.
