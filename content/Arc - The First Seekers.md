The founding members of the [[Seekers]] came together during the crisis at [[Nightstone]] in 1485 DR. Their first adventures brought them into contact with [[Felguros]], [[Zephyros]], and the wider upheaval following the shattering of the [[Giant Ordning|Ordning]].

## Major Events

- After cloud giants bombarded Nightstone, the adventurers rescued surviving villagers from goblins.
- With Felguros's help, they repelled an orc attack on the settlement.
- Zephyros offered passage north aboard [[Zephyros' Tower]]. During the journey, he shared a [[Prophecy]] concerning the restoration of the Ordning.
- In [[Bryn Shander]], the adventurers helped repel frost giants searching for [[Artus Cimber]] and the [[Ring of Winter]].

## Consequences

The party established lasting ties with Felguros and Zephyros. The crisis among giantkind became a continuing concern, eventually drawing the Seekers into the search for five ancient relics.

Many of Nightstone's rescued inhabitants abandoned the settlement; those who remained attempted to rebuild it. Its later destruction is covered in [[Arc - The Erasure of Nightstone]].
