A shop in [[Calimsharn]] selling magical items, mostly relics found in the surrounding ruins of the Desert. Its owner is a male human named [[Lord of Gold|Zafeeq el Jafar]], a stoic and ruthless vendor. His assistant, [[Gilda]], ensures that no one attempts to steal from the shop. Recently, [[Lord of Gold|Zafeeq]] contacted the [[Seekers]] with news of a newly acquired magical shield.
