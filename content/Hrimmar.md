A dwarf archmage of the [[Order of the Purple Robes]] whose specialty is transmutation. He has a pet ferret called [[Rusty]].

He wore yellow and red as a protest against the order during the crisis in the [[Luminous Cloud Citadel]].

Hrimmar said telepathically that the party should visit his tower to learn the true nature of the site, and claimed that [[Magister Lavinia Brightswan|Lavinia]] intended to rule the order alone. In his private quarters, he accused [[Eszteban, the Magnificent|Eszteban]] of refusing shared credit for the [[Discoveries]] and asked the party to deliver those Discoveries to him instead of the order, while also stating he would not share them broadly.

Following [[Eszteban, the Magnificent|Eszteban]]'s death and the formation of the Order council, Hrimmar suggested that the party remain involved in the order's future.
