Rolling-Thunder served for centuries as the elder and civic leader of [[Alkai Tor]]. He is the brother of [[Mystic Howling-Gale|Howling-Gale]]. A charismatic and steady presence, he brokered the peaceful integration of humans into the formerly all-elven village roughly fifty years before 1486 DR.

Rolling-Thunder was among the earliest victims of [[Karahl-Guhl]]'s raids; he was abducted and later found dead in the ziggurat. His disappearance and death marked a turning point in the community's fortunes.
