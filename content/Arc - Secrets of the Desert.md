The [[Seekers]]' adventures in the [[Desert]] drew them into the politics of [[Calimsharn]], the fate of [[Felguros]], and the legacy of [[Alchemy]]. Their search for relics and knowledge exposed dangers beneath the city's prosperity.

## Major Events

### Calimsharn and Askor

Felguros's capture brought him to the [[Arena of the Desert]]. The Seekers negotiated with the [[Lord of Slaves]] and entered a contract requiring occasional gladiatorial service until Felguros could be freed.

They also carried out a successful heist at [[Askor]], Felguros's former lair and a site associated with one of the giant relics. An attempted teleportation to Calimsharn instead returned them to the gates, where the [[Doom of the Desert]] warned of vengeance.

### Alkhazir and Narthezeem

The [[Emerald Tablet]] became a focus of interest for both the [[Lord of Science]] and the [[Lord of Knowledge]]. The party explored the [[Temple of Narthezeem]] and learned that [[Alkhazir|Martin]], servant of the [[Extradimensional Mansion]], was the ancient alchemist Alkhazir.

Alkhazir regained his original consciousness. The temple was destroyed, and accounts following these events describe the Seekers as scattered across the world.

### The Blood Mines

The [[God of the Desert]], whose blood supplied the city's [[Blood Stones]], began manifesting through the [[Red Warriors]]. Its threatened [[Seven Plagues]] included the worsening of the [[Red Plague]] and the explosion of the Red Warriors, devastating the city.

The [[Blood Mines Expedition]], involving the Seekers, gladiators, and the [[Lords of Calimsharn]], succeeded in destroying the entity's heart.

## Consequences

Calimsharn suffered widespread destruction and a developing power vacuum. Alkhazir rejected his former servitude as Martin, while the party's separation reshaped its active membership.

The Lord of Knowledge sacrificed himself to settle the city's debt to [[Father Rogers]]. His posthumous request led into [[Arc - The Luminous Cloud Citadel]].

## Unresolved Threads

The wider consequences of destroying the heart, Alkhazir's future intentions, and the fate of the separated Seekers remain open.
