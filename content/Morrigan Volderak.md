Morrigan Volderak is a paladin of [[Tyr]], a member of the [[Order of the Golden Lion]], and one of the [[Seekers]]. Adopted by [[High Supervisor Aelfred]] in 1470 DR, she trained under his guidance and later received her family heirloom, the sword [[Crownfall]].

She belongs to the surviving bloodline of [[Athrax Volderak]] and is hunted by [[Gorm]], who appears intent on exterminating its remaining descendants. Aelfred secured [[Tyr]]'s protection for her while she remained in [[Waterdeep]], but Gorm resumed his pursuit after she left the city.
