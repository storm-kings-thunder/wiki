Born in 1474, Scamelion was an urchin in [[Luskan, the city of Sails|Luskan]] who quickly caught the interest of [[Don Tigre]]'s clan when he grew a reputation in the streets for being able to easily steal from naive visitors. Finding out about this, [[Red, the Kid]] offered to teach Scamelion to be an even better thief and to join as one of the informants of [[Don Tigre]]'s clan.

In 1486 DR, he was the first member of [[Don Tigre]]'s clan to find out about Redd's arrival and guided the group back to the hideout. During his visit to [[Luskan, the city of Sails|Luskan]], Redd spent some time further training Scamelion before leaving in haste with his group of adventurers.

As of now, Scamelion's current activities are unknown.

When the [[Seekers]] teleported back to [[Luskan, the city of Sails|Luskan]], Scamelion met them at the docks and guided them through underground tunnels to [[Don Tigre's Hideout]].

Human Illuskan male. Alignment: Chaotic Neutral.
