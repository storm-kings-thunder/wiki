Born in 1474, Scamelion was an urchin in [[Luskan]] who quickly caught the interest of [[Don Tigre]]'s clan when he grew a reputation in the streets for being able to easily steal from naive visitors. Finding out about this, Redd the Kid offered to teach Scamelion to be an even better thief and to join as one of the informants of [[Don Tigre]]'s clan.

In 1486 DR, he was the first member of [[Don Tigre]]'s clan to find out about Redd's arrival and guided the group back to the hideout. During his visit to [[Luskan]], Redd spent some time further training Scamelion before leaving in haste with his group of adventurers.

As of now, Scamelion's current activities are unknown.

Human Illuskan male. Alignment: Chaotic Neutral.
