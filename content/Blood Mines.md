The Blood Mines lie between the outer and middle districts of [[Calimsharn]] and provide most of the city's wealth. Most residents of the outer district work here to afford basic necessities, yet rarely gain access to the [[Hospital]] in the middle district when they need treatment.

The [[Lady of Blood]] oversees the mines and maintains a dedicated force of soldiers to patrol them. These troops protect the city from abominations emerging from the [[Pit]] and contain miners infected with the [[Red Plague]].
