The Demiplane of Elminster manifests as a small, floating island suspended within the [[Astral Void]]. At its center stands a modest mansion seamlessly connected to a tall wizard’s tower. Access to this demiplane is heavily restricted — only those granted [[Elminster]]’s express permission may enter, and even then, entry requires standing at a precise location within [[Waterdeep]].

In 1486 DR, [[Elminster]] hosted a grand celebration within his demiplane, inviting the [[Seekers]] and several other notable guests. The evening’s centerpiece was to be a murder mystery, with the participants tasked to uncover [[Elminster]]’s “killer” as part of a playful magical game.

However, the event took a grim turn when [[Elminster]]’s old rival, [[Pathos Gloomblight]], seized the opportunity to enact a genuine assassination. During the festivities, [[Pathos Gloomblight|Pathos]] trapped [[Elminster]]’s soul within a gemstone, plunging the party into chaos.

Thanks to the [[Seekers]]’ investigation and intervention, the true culprit was exposed, defeated, and ultimately slain — his remains consumed by [[Connor Lycanmor]].
