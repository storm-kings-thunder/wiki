A port city and the origin of criminal elements such as [[Red, the Kid]] and [[Don Tigre]]'s mafia.

Recent reports describe a volatile geopolitical climate. The fleets of the [[Pirate Lords]] rose into a golden era and became the dominant maritime force after the recent tsunami on the coast, but the balance shifted after [[Zarobataal]] killed one of their captains.

Panic in the city contributed to factional splits among local criminal powers. One breakaway faction moved against another Pirate Lord, and retaliation escalated into alliances with the [[Red Wizards of Thay]].

Luskan is currently covered by anti-scrying wards established by the [[Red Wizards of Thay]].

The [[Red Wizards of Thay|Red Wizards]] now control most of the city's power structure and appear to be using Luskan as a test case for a colonial model of rule. [[Don Tigre]]'s faction still holds safer ground in parts of the docks and underground, while the surviving [[Pirate Lords]] are weakened, isolated, or acting behind Thayan backs.

The [[Seekers]] identify the main active powers as the Red Wizards, the weakened [[Pirate Lords]], [[Red, the Kid|Red]]'s divided family, the [[Zhentarim]], and a possible unknown faction connected to [[Illusk]].

The Thayan presence was apparently established through contracts brokered by [[Father Rogers]] after the Pirate Lords requested aid. Their Tharchion, [[Kessara Vold]], received the Seekers at the [[Administrative Tower]], defended the occupation as restoring order, and sought [[Krieg]]'s removal while offering terms to [[Don Tigre]]'s clan.

The [[Seekers]] returned to the city through a teleportation circle in a warehouse outside Luskan. They have since negotiated with [[Taerl]], reunited with [[Connor Lycanmor|Connor]] in [[Kurth's Tower]], and withdrawn to [[Don Tigre's Hideout]] before meeting Kessara the following day.
