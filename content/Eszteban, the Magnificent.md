Founder of the [[Order of the Purple Robes]] and former student of the [[Lord of Knowledge]] working across many schools of magic rather than specializing in only one.

Once brilliant, he fell into paranoia, imprisoning himself within the upper levels of the [[Luminous Cloud Citadel]]. He sealed the upper library and left a message barring other members of the [[Order of the Purple Robes]] from entry.

Correspondence between Eszteban and [[Lord of Knowledge|Professor Ishmael]] references the project of the [[Great Discoveries]], meant to assemble a major archive of spells and arcane findings. The correspondence also references his apprentice, [[Magister Lavinia Brightswan|Lavinia]].

According to [[Baelgrak]], Eszteban once sought to improve the world through magic, but his priorities shifted abruptly after an unknown discovery.

His paranoia and decline were caused by a dual physical and mental poison administered by [[Meredin]] under instructions from [[Father Rogers]]. To resist the poison, Eszteban made a pact of his own with [[Father Rogers]]. He had also discovered the origin of Ishmael's Truespeech. He stated that [[Magister Lavinia Brightswan|Lavinia]] had attempted to kill him at some point.

He was killed by [[Meredin]], who threw a dagger into his throat in the upper library of the [[Luminous Cloud Citadel]].

He contracted [[Obi diu Diva]], a genie from the Plane of Fire, to serve as a guardian in the tower's upper levels under a decade-long binding. The genie was dismissed when the [[Seekers]] used Banishment to send him back to his plane.

A portrait of Eszteban in the research room is enchanted and can animate to speak; he used it to order the party to leave the [[Luminous Cloud Citadel|Citadel]].
