[[Malar]] created six demigod-like entities of beasthood, with a seventh forming from the leftover energy. These entities formed a pact with an ancient noble family through an unknown intermediary. As a result from this pact, the sons of [[Malar]] each possessed a single individual who were used to fighting on the frontlines against a mysterious army of undead.

2. [[Ulgyr]] - Werebear
