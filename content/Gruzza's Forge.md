Gruzza is a secretive dwarf blacksmith who arrived in Alkai Tor roughly a year before the events of 1486 DR. She keeps to herself and maintains a small forge on the village's edge, producing simple but well-made tools and a few finely crafted weapons for those she trusts.

Her isolation and the presence of strange tools in her workshop made some villagers suspicious when disappearances began, but the Seekers later cleared her name when they revealed the truth behind the kidnappings.

Notes
- Race: Dwarf
- Role: Blacksmith, reclusive resident of Alkai Tor
