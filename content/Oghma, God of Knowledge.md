**Domains**: None
**Home Plane**: The Outlands
**Pantheon**: Faerûnian
**Province**: Knowledge, thought, ideas
**Symbol**: A blank scroll
**Typical Worshipers**: Archivists, cartographers, sages, teachers, and students

Oghma is the god of knowledge, thought, and ideas. He delights in sharing what he knows, but takes time to weigh threats to knowledge and consults Deneir, Milil, and Gond before acting. He usually sends an owl when he needs to reach a mortal. On rare occasions, he speaks directly into the mind of someone deep in thought.

His clergy preserve and share knowledge. Temple archivists catalogue and copy manuscripts, while travelling scholars seek out new lore. Most temples also serve as public libraries. At an evening service called the Covenant, clergy and visitors read aloud from books, share songs, and recount what they have learned. Oghma's Chosen have supernatural memories and can preserve the lore of communities whose records have been lost or whose histories are passed down orally.

The House of Knowledge in [[Neverwinter]]'s [[Protector's Enclave]] is a temple of Oghma and houses the city's largest library. It was badly damaged when [[Mount Hotenow]] erupted in 1451 DR and was restored in 1484 DR.
