A prominent center of arcane learning in [[Silverymoon]]. The party arrived via teleportation circle directly into its courtyard; knights bearing magically infused tattoos were present, and weapons must be registered at a military checkpoint near the bridge.

The Academy is governed by a council of masters, three of whom entered negotiations with the [[Order of the Purple Robes]] over a formal partnership: [[Elandar Sunshadow]] (elf evoker), [[Elustriel Dawncrown]] (half-elf diviner), and [[Varek Ironquill]] (dwarf abjurer).

The Academy was informed about [[Father Rogers]] and the events surrounding his contracts, infernal influence, and connection to [[Red, the Kid]].

## Alliance Terms Offered
- Deployment of Academy members to the Order
- Free exchange of information
- Premium magical ingredients from Academy stores
- Access to restricted archives
- Assistance sourcing rare spell components
- Monetary compensation
