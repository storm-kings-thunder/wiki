The Arcane Academy is a prominent centre of magical learning in [[Silverymoon]], governed by a council of masters. Its mages contributed to the city's defence during the [[Battle for Silverymoon]], using their mastery of different schools of magic against the invading forces.

The Academy has a teleportation circle in its courtyard, through which the [[Seekers]] arrived during their visit to the city.

## Known Masters

The following masters represented the Academy in negotiations with the [[Order of the Purple Robes]]:

- [[Elandar Sunshadow]] — elf evoker.
- [[Elustriel Dawncrown]] — half-elf diviner.
- [[Varek Ironquill]] — dwarf abjurer.

## Relations with the Order of the Purple Robes

The Academy entered negotiations with the Order over a formal institutional partnership. During these discussions, it was informed about [[Father Rogers]], his infernal contracts, and his connection to [[Red, the Kid]].

The proposed terms included:

- Deployment of Academy members to the Order.
- Free exchange of information.
- Provision of premium magical ingredients from Academy stores.
- Access to restricted archives.
- Assistance obtaining rare spell components.
- Monetary compensation.
