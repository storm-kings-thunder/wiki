The Arm and Hammer is a smithy in [[Neverwinter]]'s [[Blacklake District]], owned by the dwarf [[Bob Stevens]]. A veteran who lost an arm serving in the army of [[Mithral Hall]], Bob uses runic magic to craft powerful enchanted weapons.

In 1486 DR, [[Grothgar]] attacked Bob during his rampage through Neverwinter, costing the smith his remaining arm.
